Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, friendly teacher explaining step by step how an X-ray machine sees your bones without opening your skin. Clear, warm and curious, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers, never shouting. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, a little unhurried. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Make the opening question intriguing. Emphasize key words like الإلكترونات, مية ألف فولت, نص سرعة الضوء, تسعة وتسعين في المية, الكالسيوم, ضل عضمك, المجهول. Make the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
