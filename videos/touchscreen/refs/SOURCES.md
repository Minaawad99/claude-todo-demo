# Sources — «الشاشة بتحس بيك إزاي؟»

## Facts
- E.A. Johnson, Royal Radar Establishment (Malvern, UK): capacitive touch display, short article in Electronics Letters (1965), fuller paper with photos (1967); aimed at air-traffic control. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Touchscreen · https://billbuxton.com/inputTimeline.html
- Projected-capacitive sensing: ITO row/column electrodes, mutual capacitance at each crossing, the controller scans the grid (up to ~240 Hz touch sampling on current phones).
- Korea, winter 2010: sausage snacks used as styluses, reported sales jump.
