# Sources — «فين الـ10 لتر؟» (tankless gas water heater)

- The "10 L" rating is a flow rate: 10 litres of hot water per minute at a 25 °C rise; higher temperatures mean
  proportionally less flow (e.g. a 50 °C rise gives about 5 L/min):
  - https://www.rinnai.com.au/wp-content/uploads/HW-GasCF-Flowmaster-10-BRO.pdf
  - https://www.rinnai.com.au/wp-content/uploads/Flowmaster-10-CF-Technical-Information.pdf
  - https://www.manomano.co.uk/gas-instantaneous-water-heaters-1696
- General knowledge: the water-pressure diaphragm opens the gas valve; battery (or piezo) spark ignition; finned copper heat
  exchanger with a coil; a flue and ventilation are required for the exhaust.
