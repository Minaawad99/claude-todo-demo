# References for the ATM drawings

Wikimedia Commons search returned unrelated documents for "ATM interior cassettes" and then refused further
requests ("too many requests"), so no photos were used. The machine is drawn from the common lobby/through-the-wall
layout (NCR / Diebold-Nixdorf style):

- fascia: header with camera, screen with side function keys, card reader slot with a lit throat, receipt slot,
  encrypting PIN pad (EPP) with privacy hood (cancel red, clear yellow, enter green), cash slot with shutter;
- top cabinet: PC core, motorised card reader, thermal receipt printer;
- steel safe: currency cassettes (one denomination each), pick unit (friction/vacuum rollers), note transport,
  thickness (double-note) sensor, divert gate to a reject/purge bin, stacker wheel, presenter belts to the shutter.

Facts: Barclays archive (first cashpoint, Enfield, 27 June 1967, carbon-14 cheques); Finextra / Al Jazeera
(Shepherd-Barron's account of the four-digit PIN); Google Patents US10049525, EP0582473B1 (cassettes, picking).
