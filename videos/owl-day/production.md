# «اعترافات برّية»: البومة عمياء بالنهار

**الضيف:** البومة.
**الخلاصة:** البومة مش عمياء بالنهار. معظم البوم بيشوف في النور، وبعض الأنواع نشطة في النهار أصلاً، لكن الشمس القوية بتضايق عينيها.

## المصادر والتحقق
- Pupils contract in bright light in owls, as in humans (ABC Birds).
- Some owls are active by day or at dusk (Northern Hawk Owl, Northern Pygmy Owl per Wildlife Informer).
- Small birds mob owls by day, which means owls are visible and noticed (ties to the mobbing insert in the crow episode).
- A 2018 study linking eye colour to daytime activity is reported by ABC Birds, but the original paper was not found. **Not used in the script.**
**Needs before publishing:** a peer-reviewed source on owl vision in bright light. Current sources are popular wildlife sites.
