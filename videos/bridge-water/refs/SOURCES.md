Facts checked:
- Tahya Misr / Rod El Farag Axis bridge: Guinness World Record (May 2019), widest cable-stayed bridge, 67.3 m (Guinness World Records; Arab Contractors release).
- Washington Roebling got caisson disease in the Brooklyn Bridge caissons; Emily Roebling relayed his instructions and followed the work (history.com, Wikipedia).
- Tremie concrete is placed from the bottom with the pipe end kept buried; concrete cures under water by hydration (ASEE NCS 2016 paper; designingbuildings.co.uk).
