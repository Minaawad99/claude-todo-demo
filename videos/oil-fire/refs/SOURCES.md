# Sources — «أوعى تطفي زيت مولّع بالمية» (real-3D «أوعى» style)

- **Style reference:** the user's gas-cylinder video (photoreal kitchen, the object as a blue x-ray hologram, red «أوعى!»
  header with the key formula, word-by-word captions, source chips, a ticking checklist, a white summary card).
  Frames analysed in the session; nothing from it reused.
- **Facts:** deep-frying oil ≈ 175–190 °C (USDA FSIS); cooking oils smoke at ≈ 200–230 °C and can self-ignite above
  ≈ 300 °C; water expands ≈ 1,700× when it turns to steam at 100 °C (a 250 ml glass ≈ 425 L of steam); the safe response
  (turn off the heat, cover with a lid or a damp tea towel, leave it covered, never water, never flour, never carry the pan)
  follows NFPA cooking-fire guidance and the London Fire Brigade's chip-pan advice; Egypt's fire service number is 180.
  Written from known guidance; quick mode, not re-checked online.
- **Engine:** three.js 0.170 bundled to `real3d/three.iife.js` (MIT licence) so the page runs from file://.
- **Voice:** Gemini TTS (gemini-3.8-flash-tts, Puck, temperature 1) from `style.txt`. **Sounds:** synthesized by `audio/sfx.py`.
