## Facts checked

- You hear your own voice by air conduction and by bone conduction through the skull; bone conduction carries the
  low frequencies better, so your voice sounds deeper to you than in a recording (von Békésy; Reinfeldt et al., 2010).
- The stapedius muscle contracts just before and while you vocalize, damping your own voice in your ears.
- A microphone picks up only the air-borne sound, which is what other people hear.
- People prefer the version of themselves they are used to: their mirror image of their face, while friends prefer
  the true photo (Mita, Dermer & Knight, 1977), the same familiarity as with your own voice.
- Beethoven is often said to have used a rod held in his teeth against the piano to hear through his jawbone; widely
  repeated, not firmly documented, so the script says «بيتقال».
