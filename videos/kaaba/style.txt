Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a warm, respectful young creator taking a friend inside the Kaaba and showing him how it is washed once a year. Calm, clear and reverent, curious rather than hyped, at a natural pace of about 140 words per minute. Leave natural, unhurried pauses between sentences and at every "…", the way someone pauses while showing a sacred place; do not rush. Every question that starts with "طب" gets a gentle upward lift, then the answer follows. Stress key words like بتتغسل, بصابون, مية زمزم, ورد طايفي, دهن عود, بالإيد, بني شيبة, مرة واحدة, تلات عواميد, باب التوبة, لأ. Say "صلّى الله عليه وسلّم" fully and respectfully. Make the opening questions intriguing. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
