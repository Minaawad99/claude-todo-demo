# الكعبة من جوة — sources

Facts checked (2026-11-27):
- Washing once a year on 15 Muharram (formerly twice: Sha'ban and Muharram); Zamzam mixed with Taifi rose water, rose and oud oil, musk; walls wiped by hand with cloths; incense after. Saudipedia «غسل الكعبة المشرفة»; Sabq «مراحل غسل الكعبة»; Youm7 2025-07-10.
- Door: King Khalid, 1399 AH, about 280 kg of 99.99 % gold; sill 2.22 m above the ground. Al-Madina «باب الكعبة.. أسرار وحكايات»; Saudipedia (weight of the gold in the door).
- Interior: three teak pillars, hanging lamps, Bab al-Tawba with an inner stair to the roof, silk curtain on walls and ceiling with the shahada and names of God. Saudipedia «قائمة العناصر الداخلية للكعبة المشرفة». Older reports call the curtain red/pink; recent footage (incl. the user's reference clip) shows green.
- Old pillar from Ibn al-Zubayr's rebuilding (65 AH / 685 CE), teak, shown in the Exhibition of the Two Holy Mosques Architecture, Umm al-Joud, Makkah. Saudipedia «معرض عمارة الحرمين الشريفين»; Al-Riyadh 1528954.
