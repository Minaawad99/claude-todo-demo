What the drawings took from them:
- Derrick: dark, heavy lattice tower amidships; accommodation block and helideck at the bow.
- BOP: tall yellow frame holding annulars and ram blocks, with red hydraulic hoses.
- Tricone: painted legs, three steel cones meeting at the centre, rows of teeth.
- PDC: gold body, spiral blades, round diamond cutters along each blade.
- FPSO: long tanker hull, process modules on deck, flare tower at one end.
