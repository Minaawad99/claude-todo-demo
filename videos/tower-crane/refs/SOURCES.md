# Sources — «تعمل ونش برجي إزاي؟» («لو» + دايجرام)

- **Drawing style:** the diagram skill (isometric, close to the real thing), reference frames in `.claude/skills/diagram/reference/`.
- **The crane** is drawn from the common top-slewing hammerhead tower crane layout (quick mode, no photo downloaded):
  2.4 m square lattice mast in 3 m sections, slewing ring, cat-head apex with pendants to jib and counter-jib, triangular
  lattice jib (drawn 50 m), trolley with hook block, counter-jib with stacked concrete counterweight blocks, cab beside the
  slewing ring, top-climbing frame with a hydraulic jack, tie-in frames to the building, ladder inside the mast, red
  aircraft warning light.
- **Facts in the script** (typical values; cranes vary): concrete foundation over 100 t (e.g. 6 × 6 × 1.5 m ≈ 130 t);
  jib up to about 70–80 m; counterweight about 10–30 t; about 10–12 t near the mast versus about 2–3 t at the jib tip;
  out of service the slew brake is released so the jib weathervanes; top-climbing: a hydraulic jack raises the upper
  part and a new mast section is inserted; tall cranes are tied in to the building.
- **Sounds:** all synthesized by `audio/sfx.py`.
