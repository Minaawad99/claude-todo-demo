Speak in casual American English, like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend why a woman's heart beats faster than a man's. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, a little playful, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't pause at commas. Emphasize key words like smaller, a quarter, cup, pacemaker, three beats, seven thousand. Make the opening question intriguing and "So, what's the deal?" playful. Sound natural and conversational, never like a formal announcer.
