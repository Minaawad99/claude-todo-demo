## Facts (NTSB AAR-82-8 as remembered, not re-checked online)
13 Jan 1982, Washington National, Boeing 737-222 N62AF to Fort Lauderdale via Tampa; 74 passengers + 5 crew; de-iced, then
about 49 minutes before takeoff; engine anti-ice off; iced EPR probes read too high; first officer's doubts on the roll;
crashed about 16:01 into the 14th Street Bridge (7 vehicles, 4 motorists killed) and the Potomac; 5 survived from the plane;
Park Police helicopter Eagle 1; Arland D. Williams Jr. (46) passed the line and drowned, bridge renamed 1985; Lenny Skutnik
jumped in to pull out Priscilla Tirado. The last words come from the published transcript, shown as text, not as audio.
The livery is drawn from the green and blue on the wreckage in the CBS footage.
