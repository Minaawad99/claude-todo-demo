# Sources — حيوانات منقرضة… نحمد الله على انقراضها
1. Megalodon — Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megalodon (length estimates ~14–18 m; extinct ~3.6 Ma).
2. Titanoboa — Head et al., Nature 2009 (~13 m, ~1,135 kg, Cerrejón, Colombia).
3. Terror birds (Phorusrhacidae, e.g. Kelenken) — up to ~3 m tall.
4. Livyatan melvillei — Lambert et al., Nature 2010 (teeth up to ~36 cm, Peru, ~9 Ma).
5. Deinosuchus — ~10–12 m; bite marks on dinosaur bones.
6. Haast's eagle — Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haast%27s_eagle (extinct ~1400; Māori legends of Pouakai) → «تروي أساطير».
7. Jaekelopterus rhenaniae — Braddy et al. 2007 (~2.5 m, ~390 Ma).
8. Arthropleura — up to ~2.6 m (Davies et al. 2021).
9. Megalania (Varanus priscus) — overlap with early humans in Australia is debated → «يرجّح العلماء».
10. Arctodus simus (short-faced bear) — ~3.4 m standing; extinct ~11,000 years ago.
11. Smilodon fatalis — canines up to ~28 cm; extinct ~10,000 years ago.
12. Dunkleosteus — bony plates instead of teeth (size revised to ~4 m, 2023).
13. Meganeura — wingspan ~70 cm, ~300 Ma.
14. Pulmonoscorpius kirktonensis — ~70 cm, ~330 Ma, Scotland.
15. Gigantopithecus blacki — ~3 m, up to ~300 kg; extinct 295–215 ka (Zhang et al., Nature 2024).
