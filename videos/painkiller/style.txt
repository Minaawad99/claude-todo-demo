Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend how a painkiller pill finds the place that hurts. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Make the opening line intriguing and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
