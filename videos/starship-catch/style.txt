Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend how SpaceX's Starship came back from orbit and was caught by its launch tower. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like لأول مرة, مسكها, سبعة وعشرين ألف, بطنها, تتشقلب, دراعين, تمنتاشر ثانية. Make the opening line intriguing and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Pronounce ستارشيب, سوبر هيفي and إيلون ماسك the way Egyptians say them. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
