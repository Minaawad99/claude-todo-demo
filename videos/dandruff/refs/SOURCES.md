# «ليه شعرك فيه قشرة؟» — sources

## Facts in the script
- Malassezia (M. globosa, M. restricta) lives on almost every adult scalp; it feeds on sebum lipids and leaves free fatty
  acids (oleic acid) that irritate the scalp of susceptible people (DeAngelis et al., J Investig Dermatol Symp Proc 2005;
  Ranganathan & Mukhopadhyay, Indian J Dermatol 2010).
- Dandruff: faster epidermal turnover; corneocytes reach the surface before they separate and shed as visible clumps of
  hundreds to thousands of cells (Schwartz et al., «A comprehensive pathophysiology of dandruff», Br J Dermatol 2013).
- Treatment: antifungal shampoos (ketoconazole, zinc pyrithione, selenium sulfide), left on the scalp a few minutes
  before rinsing (AAD patient guidance). Oiling the scalp feeds the lipid-dependent yeast.
- See a dermatologist for strong redness, spread to the eyebrows/face, or no improvement after a few weeks (AAD).
