Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend why pimples show up on one face and not on another. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like حبوب, صافي, الهرمونات, سدادة, البكتيريا, الجينات, تمانين في المية. Make the opening question intriguing and a little teasing, and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
