# «ليه الحبوب بتطلع في وشك وصاحبك لأ؟» — sources

## The idea
- The hook follows the «ليش؟» short the user sent (why your face breaks out and your friend's doesn't, with the same food).
  The script, drawings and order are our own.

## Facts (checked)
- Sebaceous glands open into the hair follicle; androgens at puberty enlarge them and raise sebum output.
  Zaenglein et al., «Guidelines of care for the management of acne vulgaris», J Am Acad Dermatol 2016; AAD «Acne: causes».
- About 85% of people aged 12–24 get acne at some point (AAD, «Acne: who gets and causes»). Script: «تقريبًا 8 من كل 10».
- Comedo: sebum plus shed keratinocytes plug the follicle (follicular hyperkeratinisation). Cutibacterium acnes lives on
  almost everyone's skin, feeds on sebum and thrives in the closed, low-oxygen follicle; neutrophils are drawn in → papule/pustule.
- Twins: Bataille et al., «The influence of genetics and environmental factors in the pathogenesis of acne: a twin study
  of acne in women», J Invest Dermatol 2002 — 81% of the variance genetic. Script: «حوالي 80%».
- Family history raises the risk; stress, poor sleep and the menstrual cycle can make it worse (AAD; Zaenglein 2016).
- Dirt doesn't cause acne and harsh scrubbing irritates; chocolate is not proven; diet (high glycaemic load, skim milk) has
  only a small association (Zaenglein 2016).
- Squeezing pushes material deeper, raising inflammation and the risk of scars and dark marks (AAD «Pimple popping»).
