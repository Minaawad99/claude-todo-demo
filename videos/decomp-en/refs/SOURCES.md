# «تحلل جسم الإنسان» / How a Body Decomposes: sources (quick mode)

## Facts (checked; standard forensic taphonomy)
- **Autolysis:** without oxygen, cells stop making energy, and lysosome membranes break down. The released enzymes digest
  the cell from inside. It starts within minutes.
- **Algor mortis:** the body cools by about 0.8–1 °C (about 1.5 °F) an hour at first. The rate varies with the
  surroundings.
- **Livor mortis:** blood settles with gravity into purple patches on the lower side. These appear within hours and become
  fixed after about 8–12 h.
- **Rigor mortis:** the muscles lock once ATP runs out. It starts in about 2–6 h and passes after about 24–48 h, as the
  muscle proteins break down.
- **Bloat:** gut bacteria spread without an immune system to stop them. They produce gases (hydrogen sulphide, methane,
  putrescine and cadaverine), usually over the first days. The odour attracts blowflies from a distance.
- **Active decay:** tissue liquefies, larvae feed, and the greatest loss of mass happens here.
- **Advanced decay and dry remains:** the skeleton can last for centuries.
- **Speed:**
  - temperature is the main factor;
  - Casper's law: a week in air equals about 2 weeks in water and about 8 weeks buried in soil.
- **Myth:** hair and nails do not grow after death. The skin dehydrates and retracts.
- **The research farm:** the University of Tennessee Anthropological Research Facility (the "Body Farm", since 1981)
  studies donated bodies to improve time-of-death estimates.

## Drawings
- Version 3 (the user asked for realistic anatomy, not a rough sketch):
  - the body has 8-head proportions, a two-tone edge and hatching like reference v20, and faint landmarks (collarbones,
    chest, ribs, navel, knees);
  - the organs sit in their real places (lungs, heart, liver, stomach, colon with haustra, small intestine);
  - the muscle is a real muscle with tendons and fascicles in the cut end, plus a striated fibre;
  - the skin layers are epidermis, dermis and fat;
  - the fingertip shows the nail plate and cuticle;
  - the skeleton is a front view.
  The camera zooms continuously from the body into the cell and into the gut.
- The style is calm and non-graphic on purpose: cells, a body always under a sheet, an inset for livor, muscle
  filaments, gut bacteria, a soil cut and a simple skeleton. There is no realistic corpse.
- Same `shots.js` for both editions; `decomp-en/sketch/make.sh` switches the labels to English.
