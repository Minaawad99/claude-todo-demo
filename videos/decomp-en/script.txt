A cell stays alive only while blood brings it oxygen. Cut the oxygen, and the cell falls apart.
So what's the first stage? It's called autolysis, self-digestion.
Inside every cell, enzymes are locked in tiny sacs. Without oxygen, those sacs burst.
The enzymes spill out and digest the cell itself, like a stomach digesting food.
Meanwhile, the body cools by about one and a half degrees Fahrenheit an hour.
The blood stops moving and gravity pulls it down, leaving purple patches.

What's the second stage? Bloat.
The gut is home to trillions of bacteria, and now there's no immune system to stop them.
They eat the body from the inside and make gas, so it swells like a balloon within a few days.

Stage three is active decay. Tissues liquefy, maggots feed, and the body loses most of its mass.
