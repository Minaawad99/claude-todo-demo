What happens to the human body after death?

1. Without oxygen, a cell's own enzymes digest it; the body cools, blood settles, muscles stiffen.
2. Gut bacteria make gases that bloat the body, and the smell brings insects.
3. Soft tissue breaks down and the bones remain; temperature and place set the pace.
Bonus fact: hair and nails don't grow after death. The skin shrinks back.
