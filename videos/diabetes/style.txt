Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend where diabetes comes from and how to protect yourself from it. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like الإنسولين, مفتاح, البنكرياس, الكالون صدّي, اتحرك, تمانية وخمسين في المية, معلقة صغيرة. Make the opening line intriguing and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Say the closing prayer "ربنا يشفي كل مريض سكر" slowly and warmly, from the heart. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
