# «السكر بييجي منين؟»: references and sources

## Drawings
- **The pancreas:** "Blausen 0699 PancreasAnatomy2" (BruceBlaus, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0). Looked at only, not reused
  (`belly()` / `pancreas()` in `sketch/shots.js`): it lies across the back of the belly behind the stomach, with its head
  inside the C of the duodenum (viewer's left) and its body and tail rising toward the spleen. Lobules and the main duct are drawn on it.
- **Islet of Langerhans:** darker insulin (beta) cells in a cluster among the pale gland (acinar) cells.
- **Glucose** is drawn as a six-sided ring (its real ring shape). Insulin is a key and its receptor a lock on the cell, the
  analogy most diabetes education uses. Muscle fibres are striped red bands under a capillary.
- **People:** `fig()` with real body proportions, copied from `videos/choking/sketch/shots.js`. The flat body cuts follow
  the «طب إزاي؟» reference sheets v17 and v20 (`videos/_reference/tabizzay/sheets/`).

## Facts checked
- **Egypt:** about 13.2 million adults (20–79) with diabetes, 22.4% age-standardised, and about 62% of them undiagnosed
  (IDF Diabetes Atlas, 11th edition, 2025: diabetesatlas.org, Egypt country page).
- **Type 2** is about 90% of cases (90–95%, WHO / IDF). **Type 1** is autoimmune: the immune system destroys the beta cells. It
  isn't caused by diet.
- **Diabetes Prevention Program** (NEJM 2002, 3,234 adults with raised glucose): a 7% weight-loss goal plus at least 150
  minutes of activity a week cut new diabetes by 58% against placebo.
- **Working muscle** takes up glucose through GLUT4 moved to its surface by contraction, independent of insulin.
- **Blood holds about 4–5 g of glucose** (about 5 L at about 90 mg/dL), about one teaspoon (about 4 g). A 330 ml can of cola holds about
  35 g of sugar, about 9 teaspoons.
- **Smoking** raises type 2 risk by about 30–40% (CDC). A fasting plasma glucose of 126 mg/dL or more means diabetes (ADA).
