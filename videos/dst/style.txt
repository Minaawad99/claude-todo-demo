Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend why Egypt moves the clock forward one hour in summer and back in winter. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like ساعة, الشمس, النهار, الكهربا, تمانية, عالم حشرات. Make the opening line intriguing and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
