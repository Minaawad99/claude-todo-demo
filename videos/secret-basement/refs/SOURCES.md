# Sources — «تعمل قبو سري إزاي؟»

Style: «لو» (built like `videos/nuclear-shelter/`). Script and drawings are our own.

- Soil weight / cave-ins: OSHA says one cubic yard of soil can weigh about as much as a car (~3,000 lb); excavations
  5 ft or deeper need sloping, shoring or shielding. A cubic metre (1.3 yd³) of soil is ~1.5–2 t.
  https://osha.gov/vtools/construction/trench-fnl-eng-web-transcript · https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/trenching/about/index.html
- Lateral earth pressure grows with depth → reinforced-concrete basement walls (~20–25 cm typical residential).
- Groundwater / hydrostatic pressure: exterior membrane + perimeter drain to a sump pit and pump; relieving pressure beats
  sealing against it. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basement_waterproofing ·
  https://basc.pnnl.gov/resource-guides/below-grade-water-management-urban-sites
- Indoor humidity 30–50% to prevent mould (US EPA, "A Brief Guide to Mold, Moisture and Your Home").
- Radon: WHO — radon is one of the leading causes of lung cancer (3–14% of cases), called "the second most important cause
  after smoking" in WHO's 2009 release; levels usually highest in basements and rooms in contact with the ground;
  mitigation = sealing floor cracks + ventilation / sub-slab depressurisation.
  https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/radon-and-health ·
  https://www.who.int/news/item/21-09-2009-who-calls-for-tighter-standards-on-indoor-radon
- Rock wool (mineral wool) as acoustic insulation; an independent emergency exit (egress) for any habitable basement.
- Digging under an existing house affects its foundations: needs a structural engineer and a permit.
