# Sources — «ليه لهب الشمعة بيطلع لفوق؟»

- Hook and drawing ideas: the reference video the user sent («Tip a candle sideways and the flame still bends upward…»,
  an English sketch explainer). Only the hook idea is borrowed (tip it → still up → nothing pulls it → it's pushed); the
  script and every drawing are our own.
- Physics checked: buoyancy / natural convection of the flame plume; flame gas at roughly 1,000–1,400 °C, so by the ideal-gas
  law its density is about 1/4–1/5 of room air; the yellow light is incandescent soot; the blue base is chemiluminescence.
- Microgravity flames: NASA «Candle Flames in Microgravity» (Space Shuttle USML-1 1992, Mir 1996): flames become spherical,
  dim and blue, fed by slow diffusion.
- Falling jar: the classic free-fall demonstration (a lit candle in a dropped jar shrinks to a small dim ball, sometimes goes out).
- People: shared person kit `videos/_templates/people/person.js`.
