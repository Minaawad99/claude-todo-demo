Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend why a candle flame always points up, even when the candle is tipped on its side. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like لفوق, بيتزق, أخف, التقيل, أكسجين, كورة زرقا. Make the opening line intriguing, say "ولا حاجة… ده بيتزق" with a small dramatic pause, and make the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
