Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend how a doctor measures your blood pressure and what the numbers mean. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like مية وعشرين على تمانين, الشريان, الرقم الكبير, الرقم الصغير, الإسعاف, القاتل الصامت. Make the opening question intriguing and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
