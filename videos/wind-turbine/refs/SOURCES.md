## Facts (checked)
- Power in the wind ∝ speed³: double the wind speed → 8× the power; wind is faster and smoother higher up.
  https://cr.middlebury.edu/es/wind_group/speed.htm · https://meteor.geol.iastate.edu/windresearch/windpower.html
- Hub heights ≈ 80–120 m on modern onshore turbines («حوالي مية متر»).
- Foundation: 600–1,000+ m³ of concrete per onshore turbine (≈ 1,400–2,400 t at 2.4 t/m³) → «أكتر من ألف طن».
  https://master-builders-solutions.com/en-gb/sectors/on-shore-wind-turbine-foundation-solutions-content/
- Blades: three, airfoil section; modern onshore blades 50–80 m long.
- Rotor ≈ 6–16 rpm, generator up to 1,500 rpm at 50 Hz, gear ratio ≈ 100–120 (Nordex N149: 6.2–12.2 rpm, i = 117.3).
  https://api.commissiemer.nl/projectdocumenten/013941_3516_B5.13_Nordex_tech_specs_N149-5700_avg_conform.pdf
- Yaw: a wind vane on the nacelle and yaw motors turn the nacelle into the wind. https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Wind_turbine_design
- Cut-out ≈ 25 m/s (90 km/h): blades pitched to feather, brake applied. https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Wind_turbine_design
- Generator 690 V; a transformer in/at the tower steps it up to medium voltage (kV) — said as «آلاف الفولت» (33 kV is
  common but one source gives 3–6 kV, so no exact number). https://www.machinedesign.com/news/when-lightning-strikes-wind-turbines
- Tip speed: TSR 5–8 → tips ≈ 60–120 m/s; 80 m/s ≈ 290 km/h → «حوالي تلتمية كيلو». https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tip-speed_ratio
- Leading-edge erosion from rain at tip speed; protected with leading-edge coatings/tapes.
  https://doaj.org/article/cd57bd0c247249a88d2c85d4b579ceef · https://wes.copernicus.org/articles/3/729/2018/
- Lightning receptors in the blades conduct strikes down the tower to earth. https://www.machinedesign.com/news/when-lightning-strikes-wind-turbines
- Ladder/lift inside the tower; red aviation obstruction light on the nacelle.
