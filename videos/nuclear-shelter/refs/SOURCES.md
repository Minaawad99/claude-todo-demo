# Sources — «تعمل ملجأ نووي إزاي؟»

Structure reference: a video the user sent (a 3D back-garden fallout-shelter explainer, "but… so you add…"); the script
and drawings are our own, in the channel's cartoon style.

Facts (civil-defence guidance, e.g. Cresson Kearny, "Nuclear War Survival Skills"): packed earth has a fallout-gamma halving
thickness of about 3.6 in (~9 cm), so ~90 cm is ten halvings (about 1/1000, ~99.9%); radiation travels in straight lines, so
entrances turn at right angles; shelters need ventilation (filtered intake, hand-pumped air) and blast valves on openings;
blast doors open outward so pressure seats them in the frame; an emergency exit avoids being trapped by debris; a radiation
meter tells when it is safe to leave; the 7–10 rule: fallout radiation drops ~10× after 7 hours and ~100× after ~49 hours,
so about two weeks of supplies is the usual planning figure.
