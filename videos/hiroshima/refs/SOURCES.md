# Hiroshima — where each drawing's shape comes from

Wikimedia Commons refused every request from this session (HTTP 429, "too many requests") for over two hours, so no
photos were downloaded. The shapes are drawn from well-documented features of the real objects:

| drawing | real features kept |
|---|---|
| Industrial Promotion Hall / A-Bomb Dome (Jan Letzel, 1915) | 3-storey wings with arched windows, central 5-storey stair tower, elliptical copper-green dome; the ruin keeps the bare dome ribs and jagged walls |
| Hiroden 650-series tram (651, 652 survived and still run) | boxy body, rounded roof, three-pane flat front, single roof headlight, trolley pole, two bogies, cream over dark green |
| B-29 «Enola Gay» | natural-metal silver, round glazed nose, four engines with 4-blade props, tall rounded fin with the black circle-R used on the mission, name and "82" on the nose |
| «Little Boy» | 3 m long, dark olive drab, round nose, box-shaped tail fins |
| the stopped watch (Peace Memorial Museum) | pocket watch with hands at 8:15 |
| the shadow on the steps | the Sumitomo Bank steps (now in the museum) |
| Sadako's cranes | origami cranes on strings, as she hung them in hospital |
| lantern floating | the 6 August tōrō nagashi on the Motoyasu River by the Dome |

Sound: the series music box, clock, drone, wind, paper and water clips from `../_templates/sketch/sfx/` (see its SOURCES.md);
