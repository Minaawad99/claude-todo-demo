# Sources — «تعمل بيت للجراوي إزاي؟» (whelping box)

Style: «لو» (`videos/_reference/law/`). The topic comes from a video the user sent (an English whelping-box explainer). We
kept the topic, not its script or drawings: our order and steps differ (soggy cardboard, a draught-free tight cloth instead
of their "potty pads" wording, newspapers, soaked puppy food), and our «والغريب» fact is different on purpose (theirs has
none of the kind; ours is "don't feed a chilled puppy before warming it"). No line or drawing is copied.

Facts (checked):
- Newborn puppies can't make their own heat; the shiver reflex comes at about 2.5 weeks (AKC); full self-regulation ~3–4 weeks
  (UK Kennel Club). https://www.akc.org/expert-advice/dog-breeding/whelping-and-caring-for-newborn-puppies/
- Box temperature in week 1 ≈ 29.4–32.2 °C (85–90 °F), lowered later (VCA; Purdue). Heat source at one side so pups can
  move away. https://vcahospitals.com/know-your-pet/breeding-for-dog-owners-caring-for-newborn-puppies ·
  https://caninewelfare.centers.purdue.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Setting-Up-a-Welfare-Friendly-Whelping-Area.pdf
- Rail ("pig rail") about 4 in ≈ 10 cm off the floor for medium/large dogs (Dura-Whelp sizes 2.5–4.5 in); it stops the dam
  pinning a pup against the wall. https://durawhelp.squarespace.com/faqs · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whelping_box
- Puppies double birth weight in about 7–10 days; weigh daily (AKC, VCA).
  https://www.akc.org/expert-advice/dog-breeding/monitor-weight-improve-breeding-program/
- Chilled puppy: below ≈ 34.4 °C (94 °F) the gut stops (ileus); feeding before rewarming risks regurgitation and aspiration;
  warm slowly first. https://www.cliniciansbrief.com/article/critical-neonate-under-4-weeks-age ·
  https://www.dvm360.com/view/initially-treating-fading-puppies-and-kittens
- Eyes open ~10–14 days, walking ~3 weeks; weaning onto soaked puppy food from ~3–4 weeks; pups can't eliminate alone until
  ~3 weeks (the dam licks them) — not used in our script; socialisation 3–12 weeks; vaccines and deworming from the vet.
  https://vcahospitals.com/know-your-pet/breeding-for-dog-owners-caring-from-birth-to-weaning
- Left out: an exact rail height for every breed (it depends on the dam's size: "حوالي عشرة سانتي" is for a medium dog).
