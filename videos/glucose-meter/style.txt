Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend how a home blood sugar meter turns one drop of blood into a number. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like الشريط, إنزيم, إلكترونات, التيار, خمستاشر في المية. Make the opening question intriguing and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
