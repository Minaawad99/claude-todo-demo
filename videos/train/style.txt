Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend how a train locomotive pulls all the cars behind it and how the whole train stops. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like الجرّار, كهربا, مية طن, رملة, سلسلة, هوا مضغوط, كيلو ونص, لوحدها. Make the opening line intriguing and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
