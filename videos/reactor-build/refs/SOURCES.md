# Sources — «ابنيها بنفسك»: المفاعل النووي (a pressurised-water reactor, the type being built at El Dabaa)

| claim | source |
|---|---|
| natural uranium is ~0.7% U-235 (the kind that splits); power-reactor fuel is enriched to ~3–5% (we say "about 4.5%") | World Nuclear Association, conversion & enrichment |
| fuel pellets about the size of a fingertip; one pellet ≈ the energy of one ton of coal | [NEI: Nuclear Fuel](https://www.nei.org/fundamentals/nuclear-fuel); [WNA: fuel fabrication](https://world-nuclear.org/information-library/nuclear-fuel-cycle/conversion-enrichment-and-fabrication/fuel-fabrication) |
| pellets stacked in long tubes (fuel rods), rods grouped in fuel assemblies | WNA, fuel fabrication |
| fission neutrons are fast; water slows them (moderator) so they can split U-235 | standard reactor physics (WNA, "Nuclear power reactors") |
| control rods with boron (VVER: boron carbide) absorb neutrons; in = slower, out = faster | WNA, "Nuclear power reactors" |
| PWR coolant ≈ 320 °C kept liquid at ≈ 155–160 bar (≈ 70× a car tyre at ~2.2 bar) | WNA, PWR description; VVER-1200 ≈ 16 MPa |
| reactor pressure vessel walls about 20 cm of steel (VVER-1200 RPV ≈ 330 t, 13 m × 4.5 m) | [NucNet / WNN on the El Dabaa RPVs](https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/reactor-vessel-installed-in-el-dabaas-second-unit); typical PWR vessel walls 20–25 cm |
| primary water is radioactive, so a steam generator boils separate clean water; steam → turbine → generator; condenser cooled by sea water at coastal plants | WNA, PWR |
| containment: reinforced/prestressed concrete over a metre thick | typical PWR containments (≈ 1–1.2 m); general statement |
| the blue glow in the water is Cherenkov radiation: charged particles faster than light travels in water | standard physics (e.g. Nobel 1958, Cherenkov) |
| spent-fuel pool, emergency diesel generators, site fences | WNA, PWR plant layout |
