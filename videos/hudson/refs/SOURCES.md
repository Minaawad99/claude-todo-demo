## Facts (NTSB report AAR-10/03 and the FAA tapes, as summarised from memory; checked against the recording's words)
15 Jan 2009, LaGuardia to Charlotte, A320, 150 passengers + 5 crew; takeoff 15:24:56; bird strike (Canada geese) about 15:27:11 at
about 2,800 ft; "We're gonna be in the Hudson" about 15:29:28; ditching 15:30:43 (about 208 s after the strike); APU switched on
right after the strike; NTSB simulator runs: an immediate turn could reach LaGuardia, with a 35-second delay none did; first ferry
alongside in about 4 minutes; all 155 survived; FAA released the tapes on 5 Feb 2009; the plane is in the Sullenberger Aviation
Museum, Charlotte.
