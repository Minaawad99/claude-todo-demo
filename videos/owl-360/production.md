# «اعترافات برّية»: البومة تدور 360 درجة

**الضيف:** البومة.
**الخلاصة:** البومة لا تدور رأسها دورة كاملة، بل نحو 270 درجة، وعيناها ثابتتان في محجريهما.

## المصادر والتحقق
- Owls turn their heads roughly 270 degrees in each direction; sources (National Geographic, University of Illinois vet blog) agree the full 360 is a myth.
- Eyes are tube-shaped and fixed in their sockets (same sources).
- Johns Hopkins 2013 study on 12 owls: blood pools in reservoirs near the base of the head and jaw while the head rotates.
- 14 cervical vertebrae in owls vs 7 in humans (same sources).
**Note:** the blood-reservoir detail comes from secondary sources. Check the Johns Hopkins press release or paper before publishing.
