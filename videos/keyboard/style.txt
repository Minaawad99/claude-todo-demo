Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend why the letters on the keyboard are not in ABC order. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Say the English letter names (Q, W, E, R, T, Y) the way Egyptians say them. Emphasize key words like بالترتيب, البيانو, يتزنقوا, خمس سنين, كيو دبليو إي, مية وخمسين سنة. Make the opening line intriguing and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
