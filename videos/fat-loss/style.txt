Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend where body fat goes when you lose weight. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like النفَس, كربون, تاني أكسيد الكربون, أكتر من تمانية, سبع مرات, ميتين جرام. Make the opening line intriguing and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
