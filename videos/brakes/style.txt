Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend how a car's small brakes stop a ton and a half of metal. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like السيرفو, الزيت, أكتر من طن, الكماشة, سخونة, الإيه بي إس, أربع مرات. Make the opening line intriguing and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
