## Facts
- Pedal ratio about 4:1, vacuum servo about 3–4×, master cylinder 22 mm, caliper piston 54 mm: a 20 kg push gives about 1.7 t of clamp force at a front wheel (worked out here; typical family-car sizes).
- A 1.5 t car at 100 km/h carries about 580 kJ, enough to bring about 1.7 L of water from 20 °C to the boil (worked out here).
- ABS cycles about 15 times a second; braking distance grows with the square of speed (general knowledge, not checked against a source).
