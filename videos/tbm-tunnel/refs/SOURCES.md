# References for the TBM drawings

Wikimedia Commons refused every request in this session ("too many requests"), so no photos were downloaded.
The machine is drawn from the published layout of Herrenknecht Mixshield TBMs, the type used for the Suez Canal
tunnels (4 machines, S-958 to S-961, 13.02 m cutting diameter):

- cutterhead: spoked wheel with slurry openings, disc cutters along the spokes, scrapers on the rim, centre cutter;
- excavation chamber filled with pressurised bentonite slurry, bulkhead, man-lock (airlock) for cutter changes;
- main drive behind the bulkhead; thrust cylinders pushing on the last lining ring; segment erector arm;
- lining rings of 9 precast segments, bolted, with gaskets; annular grout behind the rings;
- backup gantries with slurry feed/return pipes; separation plant on the surface; laser guidance (VMT).

Facts: Herrenknecht project page "Suez Canal Crossing"; Sika (about 72,000 segments, 9 per ring); VMT; Tunnelbuilder.
