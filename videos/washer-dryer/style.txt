Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend how a washer-dryer washes clothes with little water and then dries them in the same drum. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like الحلة, الريش, السخان, الإنزيمات, ألف وأربعمية, خمسمية مرة, المكثّف. Make the opening line intriguing and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
