# Washer-dryer episode: references

- Commons search returned nothing usable this session (scanned documents), so the machine is drawn from the standard
  front-loading washer-dryer layout: a perforated stainless drum with three lifters inside a closed plastic tub that hangs
  on springs and dampers; the heating element at the bottom of the tub; the drain pump at the bottom front; the motor
  under the tub driving a pulley at the back by a belt; the detergent drawer top front; for drying, a fan and heater
  duct on top blowing into the drum through the door seal, and a condenser duct down the back where the steam meets cold
  water and drains.
- Hand only: «طب إزاي؟» sheet v13 (same topic). Our script, shots and compositions are our own.

Facts checked: wash water for a front loader is about 15 L in the drum (the whole cycle uses more); enzymes in detergent
(protease, amylase, lipase) break protein, starch and fat stains and work best at about 30–40 °C; surfactants hold oil in
the water; spin up to 1400 rpm; at 1400 rpm a drum of about 24 cm radius gives ω²r ≈ 5,200 m/s², about 525 g, and a rim
speed of about 35 m/s ≈ 126 km/h; condenser drying turns the steam back to water and drains it; too much detergent in a
front loader makes a foam cushion that softens the tumbling and leaves residue.
