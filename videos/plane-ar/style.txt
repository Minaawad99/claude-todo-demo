Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend how a heavy airliner stays in the air. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like أربع قوى, الوزن, الرفع, الدفع, السحب, الجناح, لأ. Make the opening line intriguing and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
