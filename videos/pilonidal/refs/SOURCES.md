# Sources — الناصور العصعصي

- Pilonidal disease overview (name from Latin pilus + nidus, "nest of hair"; hair, friction, deep cleft; young men): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pilonidal_cyst
- WWII "jeep disease": 77,637 Army hospital admissions 1942–1945 (other sources ~79,000): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pilonidal_cyst
- Laser hair removal lowers recurrence (meta-analysis of RCTs, OR 0.32): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11260285/
- Men about 3–4× women, mostly aged 15–30; risk: hairiness, overweight, long sitting (StatPearls «Pilonidal Disease», NCBI Bookshelf).
- Hair enters root-first because its cuticle scales point to the tip (Karydakis' acquired theory: loose hair, force, vulnerable deep cleft).
- Surgery: drain the abscess first; excise the sinus; leaving it open heals in weeks with dressings; off-midline closure / cleft flattening (Karydakis, Bascom cleft lift, Limberg) lowers recurrence; minimally invasive options: endoscopic (EPSiT) and laser (SiLaC).
