# Sources — أغرب شروط الزواج للرجال حول العالم

1. Hamar bull jumping (Ukuli Bula), Ethiopia — Smithsonian photo contest: https://photocontest.smithsonianmag.com/photocontest/detail/ukuli-bula/ ; travel accounts — naked run across the backs of cattle, four times; rite to manhood and marriage.
2. Sharo, Fulani (northern Nigeria) — Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharo_Festival ; The Guardian (Nigeria): https://guardian.ng/life/sharo-festival-where-men-are-flogged-for-love/ — flogging without showing pain; weak suitors may be refused. Not all Fulani practise it.
3. Sateré-Mawé bullet-ant gloves, Brazil — Smithsonian: https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/brazilian-tribe-becoming-man-requires-sticking-your-hand-glove-full-angry-ants-180953156/ — rite to manhood, repeated about 20 times. (Card says manhood, not marriage.)
4. Wodaabe Gerewol, Niger/Chad — Africanews: https://africanews.com/amp/2017/05/22/wodabee-male-seduction-contest-this-is-culture — men in make-up dance, women choose.
5. Manglik / marrying a tree, India — Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mangala_Dosha — symbolic marriage to a tree or clay pot; Aishwarya Rai "reportedly".
6. Korea, beating the groom's feet with dried fish — Korea.net: https://www.korea.net/NewsFocus/Culture/view?articleId=294270
7. Lobola, South Africa — Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lobolo — uncles negotiate, the groom does not take part directly; cattle or cash.
8. Tabua, Fiji — Australian Museum: https://australian.museum/learn/cultures/pasifika-collections/melanesian/tabua-whale-tooth-ornaments-fiji/ ; Malay Mail 2017 — sperm-whale tooth to the bride's father.
9. قائمة المنقولات, Egypt — signed list of the home's contents held in trust by the husband; withholding it can be prosecuted as breach of trust (Penal Code art. 341, خيانة الأمانة).
10. Daur chick liver, Inner Mongolia — repeated only in listicles, no ethnographic source → «تقول الروايات».
11. Tidong, Borneo, three days without a toilet — repeated only in viral pieces → «تقول الروايات».
12. Baumstamm sägen, Germany — The Local: https://www.thelocal.de/20190605/10-things-you-need-to-know-before-attending-a-german-wedding
13. الترواغ, Mauritania — Asharq Al-Awsat: https://aawsat.com/node/1255826 ; Sky News Arabia: https://www.skynewsarabia.com/varieties/1383562 — bride hidden by her friends, groom's party searches; a decoy in her dress.
14. Blackening, Scotland — Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackening_(Scottish_wedding_custom) ; Historic Environment Scotland blog — treacle, flour, feathers, paraded, day before; mostly rural/north-east.
15. Pot de chambre, France — France Wedding Style / Complete France — leftovers in a clean chamber pot, couple woken at night to drink; regional (Aveyron), "in some villages".

Left out: Mauritania's leblouh (force-feeding girls; harmful, about girls, not a groom's requirement).
