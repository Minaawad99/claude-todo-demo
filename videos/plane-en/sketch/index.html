How does a 70-ton plane stay in the air?
1Lift: from the wing2Weight: from gravity3Thrust: from the engines4Drag: from the air
4
forces on every plane in the air;
it flies when lift and thrust win
1. The engine throws air back fast, so it gets shoved forward, while the air drags the plane back.
2. A curved, tilted wing bends the air down, and the air pushes the wing up.
3. When lift beats weight the plane takes off; when all four balance it cruises steady.
Bonus fact: the engines don't lift the plane. They give it speed, and the wing turns that speed into lift.
