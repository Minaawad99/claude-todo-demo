# Sources — «إزاي النت بيعدّي البحر؟»

## Facts
- 570 cable systems in service (Feb 2025), 81 planned — TeleGeography: https://blog.telegeography.com/how-many-submarine-cables-are-there-anyway
- Subsea cables carry 97%+ of global internet traffic — NTRA presentation (BEREC 2025).
- ~17% of global internet traffic passes through Egypt; 90%+ of Europe–Asia traffic — CSIS: https://www.csis.org/analysis/strategic-future-subsea-cables-egypt-case-study
- Cables "often no thicker than a hosepipe" — DCD and others. Repeaters every ~60–100 km, powered over the cable from the shore;
  ~150 faults a year, mostly anchors and fishing; sharks filmed biting cables, wraps with Kevlar-like material (general knowledge, ICPC/Google reports).
