# Sources — «ليه المية أسقع في القلة؟»

## Facts
- Evaporative cooling through porous earthenware; latent heat of vaporisation of water ≈ 2,400 J/g near room temperature (≈ 1 °C for 0.5 L).
- Pot-in-pot cooler (zeer pot) by Mohammed Bah Abba, Nigeria, 1990s; tomatoes and peppers kept ~21 days instead of 2–3 in trials.
  https://www.echocommunity.org/resources/87ef239b-46f3-413c-b08b-d42528dae04c · https://www.scidev.net/global/?p=31969 · https://iifiir.org/en/news/briefs-accessible-and-free-evaporative-solution
- Porous clay water cooling in Upper Egypt (Qena) for millennia (secondary sources above).
