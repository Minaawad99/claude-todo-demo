- Arak shrub (Salvadora persica): drooping branches, pale fleshy oval leaves in opposite pairs, small red drupes in clusters (botanical description; Commons downloads were rate-limited, drawn from the description and the Brandberg photo titles' subject).
- Teeth: standard dental anatomy (enamel cap, dentin, pulp chamber and canals, roots in spongy bone, gum margin and papilla, the gingival sulcus); brushing at the gum line drawn the way the Bass-technique diagrams show it (brush head cut across, tips at 45° into the sulcus), matching the partner's own photo refs/product/bristles-gum.jpg.
- Product photos (refs/product/): sent by the partner (Smart Miswak / Mizuha, made in Japan) for use in this video.
- Facts: WHO consensus statement on oral hygiene (1986/87) encouraging the chewing stick; soft-bristle and 3–4-month replacement advice (American Dental Association); سواك from ساك «دلك» (Arabic lexicons).
