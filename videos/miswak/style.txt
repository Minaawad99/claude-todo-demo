Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a young, upbeat science creator explaining to a friend where the miswak comes from and how a miswak toothbrush works. Energetic and clear, friendly peer more than lecturer, with a quick, punchy rhythm of about 160 words per minute. Keep pauses between sentences very short, just a quick breath, so each sentence flows into the next; never stop at commas. Every question that starts with "طب" gets a quick upward lift, then the answer follows right away. Stress key words like المسواك, السيليكا, اللثة, سمارت مسواك. Make the opening line intriguing and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
