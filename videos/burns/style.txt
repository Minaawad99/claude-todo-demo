Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic, Cairo dialect only, like a calm, confident Cairene teacher explaining to a friend what to do when someone gets burned in front of them. Keep the exact same Cairo accent from the first word to the very last word: never drift into a Gulf (Khaleeji), Saudi, Levantine or Modern Standard Arabic accent, even in the second half. Pronounce ق as a glottal stop (قدامك = ادامك, دقيقة = دئيئة), ج as a hard G like Cairenes (جلد = gild, جوه = gowwa), and ث as ت or س. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with a little urgency on the steps and quiet amazement on the surprising facts. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like عشرين دقيقة, عميق, مية الحنفية, تلج, معجون, لأ, واحد في المية. Make the opening line intriguing and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful.
