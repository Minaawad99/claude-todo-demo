Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend what to do when someone gets burned in front of them. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with a little urgency on the steps and quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like أعمق, عشرين دقيقة, مية الحنفية, تلج, معجون, لأ, واحد في المية. Make the opening line intriguing and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
