Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend what happens to the body when you quit smoking, and why the cough gets worse first. Short, punchy phrases: each "…" is a quick beat, not a long pause, and every line lands fast like a reel. Warm and clear, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Emphasize key words like عشرين دقيقة, يومين, الكحة, الشعيرات, ميتين. Make the opening question sound like something the viewer has really wondered about, and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
