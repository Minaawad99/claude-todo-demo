Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend why scrolling on the phone in bed keeps you awake. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Make the opening "أوعى" a friendly but firm warning, as if catching a friend in the act. Emphasize key words like أوعى, النور الأزرق, الميلاتونين, الغدة الصنوبرية, ساعة ونص, لأ, هرمون ضلمة. Make the opening line intriguing and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
