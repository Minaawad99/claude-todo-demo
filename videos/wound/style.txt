Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend how the body heals a cut by itself. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like الصفايح, الفيبرين, الجلطة, القشرة, الماكروفاج, الكولاجين, تمانين في المية. Make the opening line intriguing and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Say "سبحان الله" softly with genuine wonder. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
