How does a tap of your foot stop a ton and a half of car?
1Pedal and booster2Fluid and pistons3Pads and disc4Tires on the road
1+
ton of clamping force,

1. The pedal is a lever; the booster and pistons multiply your push dozens of times.
2. Pads squeeze the disc, and friction turns motion energy into heat.
3. The tires stop the car, and ABS keeps the wheels from locking and sliding.
Bonus fact: electric cars brake with the motor and send part of that energy back to the battery.
