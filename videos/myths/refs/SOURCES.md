# Sources — خرافات صدّقناها طوال حياتنا
1. Goldfish memory — Plymouth University 2003 lever experiment; memories last months.
2. 10% of the brain — fMRI/PET show activity across the whole brain (Scientific American, "Do people only use 10 percent of their brains?").
3. Great Wall from space — NASA; Yang Liwei (2003) could not see it.
4. Lightning — Empire State Building is struck about 20–25 times a year (NOAA/NWS).
5. Bulls and red — cattle are dichromats; they charge the movement of the muleta.
6. Camel hump — fat, not water.
7. Bats — all bats can see; many also echolocate ("blind as a bat" is a myth).
8. Ostrich — they lower heads to turn eggs in the nest; never bury heads.
9. Carrots & night vision — WWII British Ministry of Information story covering airborne radar (Smithsonian Magazine).
10. Shaving — Lynfield & MacWilliams 1970: no change in thickness, colour or rate.
11. Knuckles — Donald Unger, Arthritis & Rheumatism 1998 (Ig Nobel 2009): 60 years, one hand.
12. Sugar & hyperactivity — Wolraich et al., JAMA 1995 meta-analysis; Hoover & Milich 1994 parent-expectation study.
13. Gum — passes through the gut in days (Mayo Clinic).
14. Napoleon — about 1.69 m (5 ft 7 in); "5 ft 2" was French inches.
15. Sleepwalking — not dangerous to wake, just disorienting (Sleep Foundation).
