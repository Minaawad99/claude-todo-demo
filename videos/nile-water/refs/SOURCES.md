# Sources: «المية من النيل لحنفيتك وإزاي بتتفلتر؟»

Facts checked for the script (web search, standard mode):

- Blue Nile share: about 60–62% of the flow reaching Aswan over a year; about 80–86% in the rainy/flood season (Abbay, Wikipedia; UNU; IEEE Spectrum "Squabbling over the waters of the River Nile"):
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Nile
  - https://archive.unu.edu/unupress/unupbooks/80858e/80858E05.htm
  - https://spectrum.ieee.org/green-tech/conservation/squabbling-over-the-waters-of-the-river-nile
- White Nile from Lake Victoria, about 30% of the flow at Aswan (UNU, above).
- Nile treatment in Greater Cairo: alum (aluminium sulphate) dosing, sedimentation, rapid sand filters, chlorine; algae, mainly diatoms, are the main cause of early sand-filter clogging (Egyptian study on an El-Fostat plant and Nile-water studies):
  - https://link.springer.com/10.1186/s42269-022-00754-8
- Turbidity is measured in NTU (the lab monitor in the video shows a reading going from about 12 to 0.4 as an illustration, not a real plant value).

Not web-checked, general knowledge: the Nile's length (about 6,650 km), the stages of a typical treatment plant, underground storage before distribution.
