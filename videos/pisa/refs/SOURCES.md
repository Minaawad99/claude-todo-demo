# برج بيزا — references

No photos were downloaded for this episode (quick mode). The drawings follow the tower's well-known real shape:

- **Tower** (`tower()` in `cartoon/shots.js`): white marble cylinder, a tall ground floor with blind arches and a door,
  six open arcaded galleries with a cornice band between them, a narrower bell chamber on top; the upper floors bend
  back toward vertical (the "banana" shape).
- **Duomo** (`duomo()`): white façade with stacked arcades and a gable, dome behind, on the lawn of the Piazza dei Miracoli.

Facts checked: construction began 1173; lean started after the second floor (1178); work paused about a century by wars;
finished 1372 (about 199 years); height about 56 m, mass about 14,500 t, foundation about 3 m deep on soft clay, sand
and water-logged soil; lean about 5.5° in 1990, closed 1990; lead counterweights (about 900 t in total) on the north side;
soil extraction under the north side 1999–2001 straightened it about 45 cm to about 4°; engineers rated it stable for at
least 200 years; a 2018 study found the soft soil's dynamic interaction helped it survive at least four strong
earthquakes since 1280.
