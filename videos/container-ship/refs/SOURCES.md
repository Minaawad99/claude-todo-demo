# References for the container-ship drawings

Seven photos sent by the user (visual reference only, nothing reused): an ultra-large container ship at sea (bridge about a
third from the bow, twin funnels aft), the view from a Maersk bridge over the bays, a ship at a fitting-out quay with tugs,
CHINA SHIPPING LINE, CMA CGM (Suez Canal), a ship under gantry cranes, MOL TRUST (Suez Canal).

What the drawing took from them:
- hull: dark blue/green above, red antifouling below the waterline, a raked bow with a bulb, the forecastle with a mast and
  the anchor, the operator's name in big white letters along the side;
- deck: red/orange lashing bridges between the bays, stacks of 6–9 tiers in muted colours (grey, white, brown, red, orange,
  blue, green);
- deckhouse: on the new large ships it stands about a third of the way from the bow with the funnels separate at the stern
  (MOL, CMA CGM, the ultra-large ship); the older layout keeps it aft (CHINA SHIPPING LINE).
