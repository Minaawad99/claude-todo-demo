# «ليه عينك بترف لوحدها؟»: references

- **Source video:** a 57 s English explainer the channel sent (eye twitch, motor units, Botox). Only its ideas and order
  were used. The script was rewritten in Egyptian Arabic on the series spine, and every drawing is new, in the «اسكتش» hand.
- **Anatomy, drawn from standard anatomy descriptions (Gray's Anatomy, orbicularis oculi):**
  - **The muscle:** a flat ring of concentric fibres round the eye, with an orbital part outside and a palpebral part in
    the lids.
  - **The nerve:** temporal and zygomatic branches of the facial nerve, coming in from the temple side.
  - **The eye:** the person's right eye from the front, outer corner on the left and the pink caruncle at the inner corner.
  - Wikimedia Commons answered HTTP 429 to every request during this build, so no photo was downloaded. The layout was
    also checked against the frames of the source video.
- **Motor units:** each motor neuron drives its own small group of fibres; all of them fire together in a blink.
- **Facts checked:**
  - **Eyelid myokymia:** benign. Spontaneous bursts of a few motor units, several per second, usually in the lower lid.
    Linked to fatigue, stress, caffeine and eye strain. Usually settles in days to weeks.
  - **See a doctor when:** the lid closes fully, or the twitch spreads to the cheek or mouth (blepharospasm, hemifacial
    spasm).
  - **Botox:** botulinum toxin blocks acetylcholine release at the nerve–muscle junction. The effect lasts about 3–4 months.
  - **Blinks:** about 15–20 a minute, so about 14,000–19,000 in 16 waking hours. The script says «حوالي 15,000».
- **Sounds:** the shared «اسكتش» kit (`videos/_templates/sketch/sfx/`, credits in `videos/tides/refs/SOURCES.md`) plus
  synthesized ticks, zaps and wipes from `videos/audio/sfx.py`. No music.
