# Tides episode: visual references

- **Globes:** real coastlines from Natural Earth 110m (public domain), via world-atlas `land-110m` and d3-geo's
  orthographic projection (`videos/lib/geo.min.js`). Ocean, land, graticule and night-side hatching follow the
  «طب إزاي؟» tides video (reference sheet `videos/_reference/tabizzay/sheets/v14.jpg`).
- **Earth photo for colours and the view over Africa:** "The Earth seen from Apollo 17", NASA, Wikimedia Commons
  (public domain). Looked at only, not reused.
- **Beach:** an isometric cut-away block (sand slope, layered cross-section, sea at the moving water level), a timber
  pier on square posts with a plank deck, and a white tide staff with red marks every 10 units, after the pier with
  tide gauge in the reference video. A rowing boat sits on the sand at low water and floats at high water.
- **Facts checked:** Moon about 384,400 km away; Earth about 12,742 km across; high tide about 50 minutes later each
  day (lunar day 24 h 50 min); the Sun's tidal pull is about 46% of the Moon's; spring tides at new and full moon, neap
  tides at the quarters; the Moon moves away about 3.8 cm a year (lunar laser ranging).
