# Sheikh Jaber Al-Ahmad Al-Sabah Causeway: fact sources

- Length 36 km main link (48.5 km with the Doha link), work from November 2013, opened 1 May 2019, Hyundai E&C with Combined Group Contracting, about $3 billion: Wikipedia, Middle East Construction News, Kuwait Times, CNBC Arabia, Al-Jarida.
- 1,510 bored piles across the main link; piles 33–60 m long into very dense sand; pile caps cast in the dry below sea level inside precast reinforced-concrete shell forms: SYSTRA project page, Dar Al-Handasah project page.
- Precast whole spans of 40 and 60 m, built in a casting yard and set in one piece by floating crane / launching gantry; weights quoted from 1,600 t (SYSTRA) to 1,800 t (Hewson, Hyundai E&C): SYSTRA, DLT Engineering, Hyundai E&C newsroom, Zeebiz.
- Main bridge: cable-stayed, a single 151 m sail-shaped steel pylon, eight pairs of stays, 120 m navigation channel, 23 m clearance: Global Highways, Dar Al-Handasah.
- Two artificial islands (south and north) along the causeway: Al-Anba (2015).
- Drive time Kuwait City – Subiya from about 70–90 min to about 20–30 min: Kuwait Times, Al-Jarida.
