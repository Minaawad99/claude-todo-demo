Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend why some people have curly hair and others straight hair. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like البصيلة, الكيراتين, كيرلي, ناعم, معووجة, الوراثة, الكيماوي. Make the opening question intriguing and direct, and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
