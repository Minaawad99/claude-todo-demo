# «ليه في ناس شعرها كيرلي وناس شعرها ناعم؟» — sources

## The facts in the script
Drawn from the literature below. They were not re-fetched online in this session.

- **The hair above the skin is dead keratinised cells; its shape is set in the bulb:** Thibaut S. et al., «Human hair shape is
  programmed from the bulb», British Journal of Dermatology 152 (2005) 632–638. The follicle of curly hair is curved, and
  cell division and differentiation are asymmetric across it.
- **Curly hair is oval or flat in cross-section, straight hair is round:** Westgate G.E. et al., «The biology and genetics of
  curly hair», Experimental Dermatology 26 (2017) 483–490. The curved follicle is the main cause; the cross-section goes with it.
- **Genetics:** Medland S.E. et al., «Common variants in the trichohyalin gene are associated with straight hair in
  Europeans», American Journal of Human Genetics 85 (2009) 750–755. Westgate 2017 reviews the other genes.
- **Shaving doesn't change the hair's texture:** the follicle is untouched by a razor.
- **«Chemo curls»:** hair often grows back with a different texture (often curlier) after chemotherapy, usually for a while.
  Freites-Martinez A. et al., «Hair disorders in patients with cancer», J. Am. Acad. Dermatol. 80 (2019) 1179–1196.
- **Summary card: about 1 cm a month** (about 0.35 mm a day): standard figure for scalp hair growth.
