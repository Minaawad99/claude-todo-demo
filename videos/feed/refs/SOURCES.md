## Facts checked
- Watch time is the strongest signal TikTok uses (TikTok, «How TikTok recommends videos #ForYou», 2020; WSJ, «Inside
  TikTok's Algorithm», July 2021).
- WSJ bot accounts: TikTok learned many of their interests in under two hours. The bot «kentucky_96» lingered on sad
  videos; after 224 videos (36 minutes of watching), 93% of its feed was about sadness or depression (some write-ups say 33 min).
- New videos are shown first to a small group, then wider if people watch them (TikTok's own description).
- Meta Pixel on about a third of popular websites (The Markup, «Blacklight», 2020: about 30% of the top 80,000 sites).
- Northeastern University (Pan, Choffnes et al., 2018): 17,260 Android apps tested; none secretly turned on the
  microphone or sent audio. Some sent screen recordings to analytics firms.
