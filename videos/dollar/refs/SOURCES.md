# «ليه الدولار بيغلى؟» — facts and drawings

## Facts checked (October 2026)
- Wheat imports about 13 million tonnes a year (13.1–13.2 Mt in 2025; USDA forecast about 13 Mt for 2025/26): millingmea.com, USDA FAS.
- Remittances from Egyptians abroad: about 41.5 billion dollars in calendar 2025, a record (Central Bank of Egypt via Arab Finance, Egyptian Streets).
- Monthly trade 2025 (CAPMAS): imports about 7.8–9.5 billion dollars a month, exports about 4.2–4.6 billion, so exports are roughly half of imports.
- Suez Canal revenue: about 10.25 billion dollars in FY2022/23, about 4 billion in 2024 after the Red Sea attacks (Suez Canal Authority).
- Float of 3 November 2016: from 8.88 to more than 18 pounds to the dollar within weeks.
- Float of 6 March 2024: from about 30.9 to about 49.5.
- Ras El Hekma: deal with the UAE (ADQ) worth 35 billion dollars, announced 23 February 2024, two weeks before the float.
