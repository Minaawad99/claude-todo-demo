Facts checked:
- Deposit on one-way bottles and cans in Germany since 1 January 2003; 25 cents each.
- Refillable deposit: 8 cents for glass (beer), 15 cents for PET refillables.
- Machines read the EAN barcode and check the DPG deposit mark, which is printed with a security ink detectable by the
  machine (infrared); many also check shape/contour and weight.
- One-way PET and cans are compacted on site; PET is later shredded and recycled into new bottles. Refillables go back
  to the bottler to be washed and refilled; a glass bottle up to about 50 times.
- Tomra was founded in 1972 in Norway by the brothers Petter and Tore Planke; it is the market leader for these machines.
- Return rate for one-way deposit containers in Germany: about 98 %.
