Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend how smart crows really are. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with real amazement and wonder on each experiment and surprising fact, as if you can't quite believe it yourself. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like يغطسها, خطاف, تمن خطوات, الجرة, القناع, العزا, تحقيق, هدايا. Make the opening line intriguing, ask "هو ده طبيعي؟" with genuine surprise, and say "تعالوا نعرف إيه حكاية الغراب" playfully. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
