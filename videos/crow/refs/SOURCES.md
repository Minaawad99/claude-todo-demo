## Facts (checked)
- Bread dunking by crows, dry/large food dunked more: Wilson Bulletin, doi:10.1676/04-108.1
- Betty bends wire into a hook: Weir, Chappell & Kacelnik, Science 2002 (Oxford)
- 007's eight-step puzzle: BBC Inside the Animal Mind, 2014 (he had seen each part separately before)
- Aesop's fable paradigm, sinking vs floating, 5–7-year-old children: Jelbert et al., PLOS ONE 2014
- Forebrain neuron counts like primates: Olkowicz et al., PNAS 2016
- Crows count out loud 1–4: Liao, Brecht, Johnston & Nieder, Science 2024 (NPR coverage)
- Mask experiment, young crows learn to scold: Marzluff et al., University of Washington
- «Crow funerals» as danger learning: Swift & Marzluff, Animal Behaviour 2015
- Sendai crows and walnuts: Nihei (Tohoku University); filmed by BBC; a 2024 study questions the traffic-light story, so the script only says they were filmed dropping walnuts on the road
- Corvid Cleaning, Sweden (2022 pilot)
- Quran, al-Ma'ida 5:31 (the crow showing Adam's son how to bury his brother)
