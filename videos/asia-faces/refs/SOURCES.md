## Facts checked
- Modern humans left Africa about 50,000–70,000 years ago (script: «حوالي ستين ألف سنة»).
- People reached East Asia about 40,000–45,000 years ago (Tianyuan man, ~40,000 years; early sites older).
- The founder effect: a small migrating group carries only part of the parent population's variation.
- EDAR V370A arose about 30,000–35,000 years ago, most likely in northern/central China (Kamberov et al., *Cell* 2013).
  It is carried by about 90%+ of Han Chinese («تقريبًا تسعة من كل عشرة»).
- EDAR 370A goes with thicker, straighter hair shafts, more eccrine sweat glands and shovel-shaped incisors (Fujimoto 2008,
  Kimura 2009, Kamberov 2013).
- Kamberov 2013 made mice carrying the 370A variant: thicker hair shafts and more eccrine glands (on the paw pads).
- The epicanthic fold's origin is unsettled; the cold/glare idea is one hypothesis (the script says so).
- Lighter skin at high latitudes is linked to weaker UV and vitamin D synthesis.
- The other-race (own-race) effect: people recognise faces of the group they grew up with better; it runs both ways.
