## Drawn from real references (looked at, not copied)

- **Eye cut:** "Schematic diagram of the human eye en.svg" (Rhcastilhos & Jmarchn, Wikimedia Commons, public domain) and
  "Three Main Layers of the Eye.png" (Holly Fischer, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0). Drawn to scale in mm
  (16 units = 1 mm): axial length 24 mm, globe Ø ~23 mm, cornea Ø 11.7 mm with a 7.8 mm front radius and ~0.55 mm thick
  in the middle, anterior chamber ~3 mm, lens ~4 mm thick and ~9 mm across (front radius 10 mm, back 6 mm),
  sclera → choroid → retina, ciliary body and zonules, optic nerve leaving nasal to the fovea, recti muscles.
- **Cornea close-up:** real layer order and ratios: epithelium ~50 µm (5–7 cell layers), Bowman layer ~12 µm,

- **Eye from the front:** palpebral fissure ~30 × 10 mm, iris Ø ~11.7 mm with radial fibres and collarette, pupil ~3.7 mm,
  caruncle at the inner corner; a Barraquer-type wire lid speculum and a suction ring on the limbus with its handle.

## Facts checked

- The cornea gives about 43 of the eye's ~60 dioptres, about two thirds of its focusing power.
- Short sight: focus in front of the retina; long sight: behind it. LASIK flattens the centre for short sight and
  removes a ring of tissue around the middle (steeper centre) for long sight.
- LASIK takes about 10–15 minutes for both eyes; the excimer laser runs seconds to under a minute per eye.
- Flap ~100–160 µm (femtosecond flaps ~100–110 µm, "about a tenth of a millimetre"); made with a microkeratome
  blade (LASIK) or a femtosecond laser (Femto-LASIK); it sticks back without stitches.
- Excimer (ArF, 193 nm) is a "cold" laser: photoablation breaks molecular bonds with little heat; each pulse
  removes ~0.25 µm; a human hair is ~70 µm thick.
- Eye trackers sample at hundreds to ~1,000+ Hz; the laser pauses if the eye leaves the tracking range.
- PRK: no flap; the epithelium is removed and grows back in about 3–5 days; more pain and slower recovery.
- SMILE: a femtosecond laser cuts a lenticule inside the stroma, taken out through a 2–4 mm incision; no flap.
- Candidates: 18+, prescription stable for about a year, enough corneal thickness (pachymetry, topography).
- After: rest/sleep a few hours, don't rub, antibiotic/steroid drops ~1 week, sunglasses, shields at night ~1 week,
  no water in the eye / eye make-up / swimming for about 1–2 weeks (the surgeon decides the exact times).
- Presbyopia after ~40 is the lens stiffening, so reading glasses can still be needed after LASIK.
- Odd fact: in November 1981 IBM's Rangaswamy Srinivasan etched cartilage from a Thanksgiving turkey with an excimer
  laser, the first sign it could cut tissue cleanly (IBM history; National Inventors Hall of Fame).
