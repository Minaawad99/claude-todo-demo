# References (visual only, nothing reused)

Photos looked at on Wikimedia Commons to draw the shapes:

- `Airbus_A380-841_Lufthansa_D-AIMC_First_Landing_in_Barcelona_Airport.jpg`: side view of the A380. Taken: full-length
  double deck with two window rows, rounded nose with the cockpit windows between the decks, tall swept fin, two engines
  seen under the near wing, wingtip fence, main gear bogies. The drawing uses a plain livery (white body, blue fin, no logo).
- `Lufthansa_Airbus_A380_D-AIML_2023-06-04_Ninoy_Aquino_International_Airport_p02.jpg`: the A380 on the runway (proportions
  of body to wing, engine positions, spray behind the engines → the exhaust puffs).
- `British_Airways_Airbus_A380-841_F-WWSK_PAS_2013_03_Trent_970_engine.jpg`: the intake seen from the front (curved fan
  blades, spinner with the swirl mark, thick nacelle lip) → `fanFront`.
- `Rolls-Royce_Trent_900_side.jpg`: the bare engine on its stand (big fan case in front, thin core behind) → `turbofan`.

Facts checked: A380 maximum take-off weight 575 t, wing area 845 m² (≈ 680 kg per m²); Trent 900 airflow about 1.2 t/s;
A380 rotation speed about 150–160 kn (≈ 280–300 km/h); air density at 11 km ≈ 0.36 kg/m³ against 1.225 at sea level (< 1/3);
Boeing 787 ultimate-load test (2010): wingtips bent up about 25 ft (≈ 7.6 m); air over the top of a lifting wing reaches the
trailing edge before the air underneath (the "equal transit time" story is wrong).
