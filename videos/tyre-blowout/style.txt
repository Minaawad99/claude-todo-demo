Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend what to do when a car tyre blows out on the highway. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like طاخ, فرامل, تقلب, بتتفرطح, لقدام, تلف, دوغري, بالراحة, خمسين, تاريخ ميلاده. Make the opening line intriguing and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
