# References for the monorail drawings

Fourteen photos sent by the user (visual reference only, nothing reused): the Cairo monorail (East of Nile line) on its
guideway over the road, its front, an elevated station and its platform with screen doors, the piers and beams during
construction, the underside of straddle-monorail cars (load, guide and stabilising tyres, power rails along the beam), a
car on a maintenance beam, and a Disney Mark VI car.

What the drawing took from them:
- the Cairo train: white body, a black wrap-around mask over the windscreen running back as a continuous black window band,
  teal / green / yellow geometric outline graphics on the lower body, grey gangway bellows between cars, skirts wrapping the beam;
- the guideway: a pale concrete beam on round columns with a wide hammerhead cap, some columns wrapped in ads, in the
  middle of the road;
- the bogie: pneumatic load tyres on top of the beam, horizontal guide tyres on its upper sides, stabilising tyres lower down,
  power rails in conduits along the beam's side;
- the station: an elevated hall with a curved roof and platform screen doors.

Facts: Innovia Monorail 300 (Bombardier, now Alstom); East of Nile line New Administrative Capital ⇄ Nasr City (~56 km),
6th of October line (~42 km); Wuppertal Schwebebahn (1901), Tuffi the elephant (July 1950).
