# Sources — «ابنيها بنفسك»: بيت في الصحرا

| claim | source |
|---|---|
| summer afternoons in Egypt's deserts can reach ~45 °C | Egyptian Meteorological Authority climate normals (Western Desert, Upper Egypt) |
| thick earth walls absorb heat by day and pass it inside hours later (thermal mass, time lag of several hours); inside stays much cooler | [AramcoWorld, "Keeping Cool"](https://archive.aramcoworld.com/issue/199503/keeping.cool.htm); [ASHRAE 2020 paper](https://ashrae.org/file%20library/conferences/specialty%20conferences/2020%20building%20performance/papers/d-bsc20-c076.pdf) |
| domes and Nubian vaults of mud brick built without timber or formwork | [Wikipedia: Nubian vault](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nubian_vault) |
| hot air rises into the dome | basic physics (convection) |
| the malqaf: a wind catcher on the roof facing the prevailing wind, bringing air down into the house | traditional Egyptian architecture (Cairo houses; Hassan Fathy, *Natural Energy and Vernacular Architecture*) |
| small windows with mashrabiya screens: air and light in, direct sun out | traditional Egyptian architecture |
| a central courtyard stays shaded and keeps the cool night air | traditional courtyard house |
| rare but violent flash floods in the desert; build on a raised stone base | Egyptian flash-flood studies (Sinai, Red Sea, Upper Egypt) |
| solar-powered pumps for desert wells | common practice in Egypt's new desert reclamation |
| Hassan Fathy designed New Gourna near Luxor, 1945–1948: sun-dried mud brick, thick walls, domes and vaults (more than 70 years ago) | [Wikipedia: Hassan Fathy](https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Hassan_Fathy); [Senses Atlas](https://www.sensesatlas.com/hassan-fathy-building-with-the-people-in-new-gourna/) |
| white walls reflect the sun | basic physics (albedo) |
| mud brick (adobe): clay-rich mud + sand + straw + water, trodden, moulded in wooden forms, sun-dried ~1–3 weeks, not fired; straw stops cracking and holds the brick together | [Wikipedia: Mudbrick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mudbrick); Hassan Fathy, *Architecture for the Poor* |
| thicker earth walls delay and soften the heat more (more mass = longer time lag) | AramcoWorld; ASHRAE 2020 (above) |
| Nubian vault: courses of bricks laid leaning against an end wall, each stuck to the last with mud mortar, no timber support; the arch keeps every brick pressing on its neighbours and carries the weight down to the walls | [Wikipedia: Nubian vault](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nubian_vault) |
| a fridge without electricity: the pot-in-pot (zeer) cooler, a clay pot inside a bigger one with wet sand between; evaporation carries the heat away, food stays fresh for days | [Wikipedia: Pot-in-pot refrigerator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pot-in-pot_refrigerator) (Mohammed Bah Abba's design; the Egyptian زير/قلة keep water cool the same way) |
