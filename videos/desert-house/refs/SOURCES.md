# Sources — «تعمل بيت في الصحرا إزاي؟» («لو» + دايجرام)

- **Mud-brick walls:** thick earth walls (about 50 cm) have a time lag of roughly 8–12 hours, so midday heat reaches the
  inside at night (thermal mass, standard building-physics values).
- **Desert day–night swing:** often 15–20 °C or more in hot deserts.
- **Domes, wind catchers (malqaf), courtyards, mashrabiya:** traditional Egyptian and Middle-Eastern passive cooling;
  evaporative cooling with wet clay jars at the malqaf opening.
- **Zeer pot-in-pot cooler:** clay pot inside a larger clay pot with wet sand between; popularised by Mohammed Bah Abba
  (Nigeria, 1990s); keeps vegetables fresh for days to weeks longer.
- **Hassan Fathy:** Egyptian architect; New Gourna village on the west bank at Luxor (1946–1952), built of mud brick with
  domes and vaults and no timber roofs.
