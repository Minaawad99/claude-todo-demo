# «عربية نقل الفلوس» — sources and references

The idea comes from the TikTok video the user sent (an English 3D build-along: pickup → armoured truck). Our script is our own,
in Cairo Egyptian Arabic, in the «لو» shape; no line is translated from theirs. Avoided on purpose: their "finders keepers"
surprise (we use the ink cases instead), their "or just use a credit card" joke, their order of steps (we start from a ربع نقل
whose bed gets a closed steel box, then the glass, the steel, and the move to a truck chassis).
