Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend why a short nap wakes you up fresh but a one-hour nap leaves you groggy. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like ربع ساعة, مدروخ, السلم, النوم العميق, خمول النوم, تسعين دقيقة, ناسا, أربعة وخمسين في المية. Make the opening line intriguing and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
