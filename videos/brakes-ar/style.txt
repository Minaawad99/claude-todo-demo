Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend how a small push on the brake pedal stops a heavy car. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like طن ونص, حرارة, الباكم, زيت الفرامل, الطارة, التيل, الكاوتش, لأ. Say "إيه بي إس" as the letters A B S. Make the opening line intriguing and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
