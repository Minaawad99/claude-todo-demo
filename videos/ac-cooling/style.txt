Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, friendly teacher explaining step by step how an air conditioner cools a room by moving heat outside. Clear, warm and curious, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers, never shouting. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, a little unhurried. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Make the opening line intriguing. Emphasize key words like الفريون, خمسين تحت الصفر, المبخّر, الكمبروسر, سبعين درجة, دايرة مقفولة, شيّال. Make the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
