Facts (checked):
- Plasma ≈ 55% of blood volume; ≈ 92% water, the rest proteins (albumin, globulins, clotting factors), salts, glucose, hormones.
- Red cells are denser than plasma, so they settle; a centrifuge separates them in minutes.
- Apheresis returns red cells with saline; citrate anticoagulant can cause tingling around the lips (citrate binds calcium).
- A plasma donation takes about an hour; plasma volume is restored within about 24–48 h.
- Interval: often every 2 weeks (European guidance; some countries allow more often) vs about 3–4 months for whole blood.
- Iron: plasma donation removes little iron because the red cells are returned.
- «More than 1,000 donations a year for one haemophilia patient»: PPTA (plasma industry association) figure.
