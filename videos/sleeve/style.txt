Warm and clear, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Brisk, energetic pace like a quick social-media explainer: keep the momentum, move straight from one sentence to the next with only a very short breath, and don't stop at commas. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
