## Facts checked
- Autorefractors measure with near-infrared light reflected from the retina (Hartmann–Shack / Scheiner-principle sensors).
- The fixation target (a hot-air balloon or a house at the end of a road) is shown far away and "fogged" (blurred toward
  plus) so accommodation relaxes; instrument myopia is the known error when it doesn't.
- Cycloplegic drops (cyclopentolate, atropine) block the muscarinic receptors of the ciliary muscle and the iris sphincter:
  no accommodation, wide pupil, for hours (cyclopentolate up to ~24 h, atropine days). Used most in children and young people.
  Mydriatic / cycloplegic bottles carry a red cap (the AAO colour code).
- Retinoscopy: the examiner sweeps a streak of light and puts lenses in front of the eye until the red reflex stops moving
  ("neutralization").
- Dilated fundus exam shows the retinal vessels and the optic disc directly; it detects diabetic retinopathy,
  hypertensive changes and papilloedema.
- Hermann von Helmholtz described the ophthalmoscope in 1851: a stack of glass plates reflecting a lamp's light into the eye.
