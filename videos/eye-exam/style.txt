Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend how the eye doctor finds your glasses number with the machine. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like البالونة, تحت الأحمر, العضلة, القطرة, الوهج الأحمر, السكر والضغط, ألف تمنمية واحد وخمسين. Make the opening question direct and intriguing, and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
