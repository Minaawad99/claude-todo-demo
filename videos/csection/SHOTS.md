## STYLE (يتحط في أول كل برومبت صورة)
```
Vertical 9:16 medical illustration, clean modern 2D digital painting with soft shading and subtle gradients,
dark charcoal-teal background (#14191c) with a soft vignette, educational anatomy style like a high-end
medical explainer animation. Anatomical cross-section colors: skin = thin beige-pink line, fat = warm yellow
bubbly layer, fascia = thin pale-white band, muscle = deep red-pink striated layer, peritoneum = thin light
membrane, uterus = thick glossy rose-red muscular wall. Baby = soft pink, peaceful, smooth, cute but realistic
proportions. Non-graphic, no blood splatter, gentle and calm. No text, no letters, no labels, no watermark.
Leave the top 20% of the frame calm and dark for captions.
```

## MOTION STYLE (يتحط في آخر كل برومبت حركة)
```
Slow, smooth, gentle motion. Static or very slow camera. No new objects appear, no text,
keep the illustration style and colors exactly the same. Calm medical explainer animation.
```
