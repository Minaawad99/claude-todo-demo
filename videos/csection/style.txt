Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend what really happens inside the operating room during a C-section. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like القيصرية, البنج, الجلد, الدهون, الرحم, والبيبي خرج, أربعين لستين دقيقة. Make the opening question intriguing, and say "والبيبي خرج!" with real joy. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
