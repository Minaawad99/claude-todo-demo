Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend why your leg goes numb and prickles after sitting too long on the toilet. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like العصب الوركي, أتخن عصب, أكسجين, إشارات, إبر, شلل التواليت. Make the opening line intriguing and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
