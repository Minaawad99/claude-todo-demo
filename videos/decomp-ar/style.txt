Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend what happens to the human body after death, stage by stage, as forensic science. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like أكسجين, الهضم الذاتي, الانتفاخ, البكتيريا, الهيكل العضمي, لأ. Keep a respectful, calm, scientific tone, never gruesome. Make the opening line intriguing and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
