# Sources — حاجات لقوها من تيتانيك بعد الغرق

1. Astor watch — Henry Aldridge & Son: https://henryaldridge.com/about-us/news/astor-watch ; CTV: https://www.ctvnews.ca/world/article/gold-watch-worn-by-richest-passenger-aboard-titanic-sells-for-record-breaking-us15-million/ — found on his body, sold April 2024 for £1.175m (~US$1.48m), record for Titanic memorabilia.
2. Hartley violin — NPR: https://www.npr.org/sections/thetwo-way/2013/10/19/237715869/violin-said-to-have-been-on-the-titanic-sells-for-1-5m ; ITV: https://itv.com/news/2013-10-19/titanic-violin-sold-for-900-000-smashes-auction-record — resurfaced 2006 in an attic in England (Yorkshire), sold 2013 for £900,000 hammer.
3. Stopped pocket watches — Encyclopedia Titanica: https://www.encyclopedia-titanica.org/a-time-to-remember-titanic-watches.html ; RMG (Robert Douglas Norman, stopped 3:07): https://www.rmg.co.uk/collections/objects/rmgc-object-200743 ; Times of Israel (Sinai Kantor): https://www.timesofisrael.com/hebrew-pocket-watch-frozen-in-time-of-titanic-wreck-to-be-auctioned/ . Times differ per watch, so the card doesn't name a time.
4. Saalfeld perfume — Science Center / exhibition accounts: https://coxsciencecenter.org/node/306 ; Encyclopedia Titanica thread: https://www.encyclopedia-titanica.org/community/threads/18423/ — 62–65 vials, recovered 2000, still smell. Card says "more than 60".
5. Shoes in pairs — NPR (Ballard, tannin): https://www.npr.org/sections/krulwich/2012/04/14/150581903/the-strange-persistence-of-shoes-at-sea ; 2004 NOAA/IFE photo.
6. Purser's bag 1987 — Bay News 9: https://baynews9.com/fl/tampa/news/2013/1/14/jewels_of_well_to_do ; Laughing Place: https://www.laughingplace.com/w/articles/2013/03/08/exploring-orlando-titanic-the-experience-jewels-of-the-titanic-exhibit/
7. Howard Irwin / Henry Sutehall — Encyclopedia Titanica: https://www.encyclopedia-titanica.org/tragic-friendship.html ; RMS Titanic Inc.: https://rmstitanicinc.com/blog/titanic-love-letters-across-the-atlantic/ — Irwin didn't sail, Sutehall took his trunk, recovered 1993 (≈81 years), love letters from Pearl Shuttle, clarinet.
8. Deck chair — Paul Fraser Collectibles: https://www.paulfrasercollectibles.com/blogs/memorabilia/titanic-wreck-deckchair-makes-149-500-in-april-18-auction — picked up by Mackay-Bennett, sold April 2015 ~£100,000.
9. Diana of Versailles — AP/ADN: https://adn.com/nation-world/2024/09/04/titanic-divers-find-long-sought-statue-amid-signs-of-accelerating-ship-decay ; Hyperallergic: https://hyperallergic.com/titanic-expedition-uncovers-long-lost-statue-of-a-woman — last photographed 1986, rediscovered 2024 (38 years).
10. The Big Piece — Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Big_Piece ; Tampa Bay Times 1996: https://www.tampabay.com/archive/1996/09/04/critics-fueled-by-failed-attempt-to-raise-titanic/ — 1996 lift failed, raised Aug 1998, 15 tons on display at the Luxor, Las Vegas.
11. Halomonas titanicae — Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halomonas_titanicae ; CSM: https://csmonitor.com/Science/2010/1207/New-species-of-bacteria-devouring-Titanic-wreck — described Dec 2010; contributes to corrosion (with other bacteria).
12. Doll's head — Encyclopedia Titanica thread: https://www.encyclopedia-titanica.org/community/threads/the-famous-dolls-head.18396/latest — Ballard 1986, first took it for a human head; owner unknown.

Left out: the champagne bottle (brand and "drinkable" claims mix Titanic with the Jönköping wreck) and the crow's-nest bell (which bell it is is disputed).
