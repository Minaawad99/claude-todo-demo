Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm but urgent young man teaching a friend what to do when someone next to him gets an electric shock. Serious, clear and confident, friendly peer more than lecturer, with a steady, punchy rhythm of about 165 words per minute. Keep pauses between sentences very short, just a quick breath; never stop at commas. The opening line is tense and gripping; "اوعى تمد إيدك!" is a sharp, loud warning; the next line is reassuring. Read every "don't" line ("ماترميش", "ماتسحبهوش", "ماتحطش", "ماتشيلش") firmly. Stress key words like ماتلمسوش, اقطع الكهربا, القلب, يرتعش, ناشفة, ضغط عالي, واحد اتنين تلاتة, المستشفى. Read the numbers clearly. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
