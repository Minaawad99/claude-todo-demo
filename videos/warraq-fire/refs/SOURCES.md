# «حريق دائري الوراق» — sources

## Facts (checked 26 Nov 2026; the investigation is still open)
- Wednesday 25 Nov 2026, evening, on the Ring Road above the «نفق عشرة» underpass, Warraq / Imbaba, Giza.
- Preliminary prosecution inspection: the driver of a truck carrying petroleum products lost control, it hit the concrete
  barrier and overturned; fuel spilled and caught fire (a survivor: in under half a minute). The Ministries of Transport and
  Electricity denied any falling power cable. Some reports spoke of gas cylinders; the script says «مواد بترولية» as the prosecution does.
- Toll: 30 dead (Ministry of Health); injured 15–25 depending on the source; 21 cars burnt (Youm7), 27 (prosecution) → «أكتر من عشرين».
- Pages: Masrawy (25 Nov, «المعاينة الأولية تكشف سبب حريق دائري الوراق»), El Watan news/details/8404079, Youm7 2026/11/25/7632572,
  Ahram Gate News/5991743, Youlyou 103122 (survivor) and 103090, 103048, Egypt Telegraph 299350, Sada El Balad 7194549, Manassa 35134, Sky News Arabia 1907121.

## First aid (standard guidance: stop–drop–roll; cool running water about 20 minutes; no toothpaste, butter or ice; don't
pull clothes stuck to a burn; cover with a clean cloth; stay low under smoke; keep upwind and far from a burning tanker).
Egypt emergency numbers: fire brigade / civil protection 180, ambulance 123.
