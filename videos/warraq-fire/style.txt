Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), as a calm, sad storyteller narrating a real tragedy: the Ring Road fire in Warraq, Cairo, where thirty people died. Slow, low and warm, with real grief in the voice but never melodramatic, about 130 words per minute. Leave short, heavy pauses after the lines that tell what happened ("وانقلبت", "ملحقوش ينزلوا", "تلاتين روح راحت"). When the script moves to "طب لو إنت في نفس الموقف", become steadier and clearer, like a caring friend giving instructions, still calm. Stress key words like البخار, ابعد, مية وتمانين, مية جارية, اتصل. Say the final prayer softly and slowly. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
