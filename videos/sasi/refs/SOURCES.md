# «ساسي» — sources

## Facts (checked)
- SASI (single anastomosis sleeve ileal) bypass: a sleeve gastrectomy, then one side-to-side join between the antrum and the ileum, with no division of the duodenum; first described as a series of 50 patients with one-year follow-up by Mahdy and colleagues (Obesity Surgery). Food passes both through the pylorus and through the join.
- Case-matched multicentre study, 58 vs 58: excess weight loss at 12 months 72.6% after SASI vs 60.4% after sleeve; operations longer (108.7 vs 92.8 min).
- A retrospective cohort (sleeve, OAGB, SASI) found a higher long-term complication rate after SASI; long-term data are still limited.
- From general knowledge, to confirm: the ileum is joined about 250 cm from the ileocaecal valve; Dr Tarek Mahdy (Mansoura University) published it in 2016. The user approved the script with these lines.
