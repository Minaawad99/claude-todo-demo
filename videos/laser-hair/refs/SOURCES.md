# «إزاي الليزر بيشيل الشعر؟» — sources

## Facts in the script
- Selective photothermolysis: melanin absorbs the pulse and heats the hair and its bulb (Anderson & Parrish, Science 1983;
  laser hair removal reviews, e.g. Gan & Graber, Dermatol Surg 2013).
- Pulse lengths of a few to ~100 ms, shorter than a blink (~100–400 ms).
- Treated hairs shed about 1–3 weeks after a session; 6–8 sessions a few weeks apart, because the laser works best on
  hairs in the growth phase (anagen), and only part of the hair is in anagen at any time (anagen / catagen / telogen).
- The FDA clears it as «permanent hair reduction», not permanent removal; regrowth is finer and lighter.
- Dark skin: longer wavelengths (Nd:YAG 1064 nm) pass the epidermal melanin and reach the follicle with less skin heating.
- White, grey and light blonde hair has little or no melanin, so it doesn't respond.

  phases: anagen full depth, catagen shrinking upward, telogen club hair with the papilla resting below) after standard
  textbook and Wikipedia «Hair follicle» diagrams. Drawn in our own hand.
