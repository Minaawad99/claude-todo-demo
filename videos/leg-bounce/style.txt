Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend why bouncing your leg while sitting is actually good for your blood. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like تلات ساعات, تلتين, السمانة, صمامات, القلب التاني, لأ. Make the opening line intriguing and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
