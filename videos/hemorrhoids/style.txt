Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend what hemorrhoids are, what causes them, the warning signs, and what happens in each operation. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers, and a light knowing smile on the opening line. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like مخدات, الحزق, دم, الخمسة وأربعين, أستك, الاستئصال, الدباسة, الليزر. Make the opening line intriguing and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
