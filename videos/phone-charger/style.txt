Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend why you should not leave your phone on the charger all night, and what it does to the battery. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like مية في المية, السخونية, تمانين, الشنطة, تنفخ. Make the opening line intriguing and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
