## Facts checked
- Essential fat: men 2–5 %, women 10–13 % (American Council on Exercise body-fat categories; McArdle, Katch & Katch).
- Energy cost of one pregnancy ≈ 77,000 kcal (Butte & King, Public Health Nutrition 2005; the older Hytten estimate is
  ~80,000). A Big Mac is ~560 kcal → ~137 burgers, so «أكتر من 130».
- Women keep more fat on the hips and thighs (gluteofemoral fat), and it is drawn on during breastfeeding
  (Rebuffé-Scrive et al., J Clin Invest 1985).
- Leptin is made by fat cells in proportion to fat mass and tells the hypothalamus how much energy is stored; low leptin
  with low energy availability switches off the cycle (functional hypothalamic amenorrhea).
- Low estrogen from amenorrhea causes bone loss even in young women (female athlete triad / RED-S literature).
- The odd fact: Welt et al., NEJM 2004 ("Recombinant human leptin in women with hypothalamic amenorrhea"): 8 women,
  3 had ovulatory cycles on leptin alone.
- Bodybuilders reach ~4 % body fat only for the days around a contest (peak-week studies, e.g. Rossow et al. 2013).
