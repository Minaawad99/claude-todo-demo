## المصادر والتحقق
Athena was often shown with an owl; the myth comes from that symbolism.
- Great grey owls (2013) failed a string-pulling test that several other bird species passed. Critics say this test is narrow, so the result is not a full verdict.
- Corvids (crows, ravens) are generally considered the most intelligent birds.
- Owl cognition studies are few compared with parrots and corvids (National Audubon Society scientist, as quoted in the search sources).
- Tool-like behaviour in some owls is reported, but rare. **Needs a primary source before we show it on screen.**
