# Sources — «القهوة بعد الأكل» (دايجرام)

- **Iron absorption trial:** Morck TA, Lynch SR, Cook JD. Inhibition of food iron absorption by coffee. Am J Clin Nutr
  1983;37:416–420. A cup of coffee with a hamburger meal cut non-haem iron absorption by about 39% (tea by about 64%);
  coffee taken 1 hour before the meal had no effect, 1 hour after cut it as much as with the meal; stronger coffee cut it more.
- **Colon:** Brown SR, Cann PA, Read NW. Effect of coffee on distal colon function. Gut 1990;31:450–453 (about 29% of
  subjects felt an urge to defecate); Rao SS et al. 1998 (colonic motor response within minutes).
- **Caffeine half-life:** about 5 hours on average in healthy adults (range roughly 1.5–9.5 h).
- **Vitamin C:** ascorbic acid increases non-haem iron absorption (widely established).
