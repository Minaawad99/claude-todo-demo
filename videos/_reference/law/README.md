# «لو» style reference — 14 videos the user sent (series «ابنيها بنفسك!», Gulf Arabic)

These are the model for every «لو» video: the drawing, the screen and the way the script is written. Ours are in casual
Cairo Egyptian Arabic instead of Gulf Arabic, with the «إيه الحكاية؟» brand. Never copy a drawing or a script line;
look, then draw and write our own. `videos/rNN.mp4` (720×1280), `sheets/rNN.jpg` (16 frames each), `transcripts/rNN.txt`

## The look (measured from the frames)

- **Full-bleed 9:16**, no black frame, no title bar, no panel: the picture is the whole screen.
- **"2.5D" vector illustration**: flat shapes with soft gradients, inner shading and soft drop shadows, so objects read as
  solid ("مجسم شوية") without being a 3D render. Thin dark-grey outlines. Glass shows highlights; metal has a light streak.
- **One scene in cross-section**: the sky (light blue to warm haze, a big soft sun glow top right, puffy clouds), a faded
  far background (city, trees, palms, desert), the ground line with grass, then the earth cut open below (a deep brown
  soil texture with grain or stones, layers getting darker). Buildings are cut open to show inside.
- **The build grows on screen**: each fix arrives with motion (lowered on a crane hook, poured, slid in, drawn along a
  path), and stays. Parts that matter get a yellow highlight ring; failures get a red ✗ in a circle and sometimes a red
  pill word («يطيح!») or the scene darkening («خربانة!»).
- **Camera**: constant slow zoom/pan inside the one scene; push in close on a detail (a valve, a hinge), pull back out to
  the whole build. Cuts are rare; cross-zooms connect the shots.
- **Captions: ONE word at a time**, big, white, heavy Arabic sans with a dark outline and soft shadow, centred, fixed height
  (around 30–35% or 65–70% of the frame), changing exactly with the voice.
- **Labels**: small dark rounded pills with white text naming a part ("ذراع الرافعة", "برج التبريد"), with a little pointer.
- **People**: a man drawn in real body proportions (flat vector, simple face), who does the actions: walks, digs, climbs the
  ladder down and up, lifts the bed, hides, stands proudly at the end. In ours he reacts to every step (see the skill).
- **Brand**: a small badge in a corner (theirs «ابنيها بنفسك!»; ours «إيه الحكاية؟»).
- Sometimes a real photo/footage at the end with "لقطة حقيقية" and a credit line.

## The script shape (from the transcripts)

1. «لو <the naive version>، يبقى عندك <the thing>.» (a statement, sometimes a question)
2. «بس المشكلة إن …» → «عشان كده بت… …» → «وده اسمه …» (names the part), repeated 5–8 times.
3. One «والغريب إن …» surprising fact or story, with a number.
4. «وكده فاضل عليك …» a list of finishing touches.
5. «ومبروك عليك، عملت <the thing> بنفسك.»
Short sentences, «إنت» all the way through, a real number in most fixes. They have a mid-video plug ("الرابط في البايو"); we
don't. Our last line stays «قولولنا، عايزين تعرفوا حكاية إيه المرة الجاية؟».
