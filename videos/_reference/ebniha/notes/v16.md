## 4. Recurring devices
- Ground cross-section in every shot (soil band never leaves, even under the kitchen).
- Dark rounded pill tags with emoji + 1–2 white words, centred on the object (3 times), plus an emoji-only tag.
- Thermometer with two numbers; calendar and clock icons for time passing.
- Colour-coded arrows: blue = cold, orange = heat. No ✓/✗, no popup cards, no molecule cutaway.
- Winter scene and a night tint used once each as "time" markers.

## 7. Camera and motion
- One continuous scene; the only hard cuts are to the winter lake and the night close-up. Otherwise slow eased pushes/pans (1–2 s) between beats and static holds while narrating.
- Parts appear by fade + slight scale pop; pipes draw along their path; ice melts by scale-down; arrows loop.

## 8. What works / weakness
1. Historical ladder (pit → ice house → icebox → compressor): each stage is a real thing, so every «بس» is believable.
2. Same four food props in every stage give continuity and a visible stake (milk goes green).
3. Colour logic is strict: orange is always heat, blue always cold, so the refrigerant loop reads without labels.
- Weakness: the compressor/condenser/valve part (0:47–1:15) packs four ideas into near-identical frames with no part labels, and the pill tags there use emoji that are hard to decode («🔥 → 🔍»).
