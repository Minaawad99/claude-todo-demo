# v18 — المسبح (swimming pool) · 80 s

## 1. Topic and look
- Builds a backyard pool: hole + water → rebar and shotcrete → hydrostatic valve → pump and sand filter → skimmer, main drain, return jets → chlorine → tiles, ladder, diving board.
- 2.5D: 3D-rendered pool shell and soil block (textured, slightly from above) in front of a flat 2D villa backdrop.
- Palette: rich textured brown soil (lower 55–60% of frame), saturated green lawn, cream villa with blue arched windows, blue sky, grey concrete, white pipes, teal filter tank, navy groundwater.
- Setting: Gulf/Saudi villa yard («حوش بيتك») with palms, crenellated roofline, flower pots. No people.

## 2. Opening (0–3 s)
- Frame 0: villa across the top third, lawn, a shallow hole already being dug with a small shovel flicking dirt, caption «لو».
- Hole deepens to a stepped pool profile in 1.5 s, a hose stream fills it, and at 3 s confetti pops with «مبروك، صار عندك مسبح!» — a fake ending in the hook.

## 4. Recurring devices
- Ground cross-section is the main stage (over half the frame is soil).
- White arrows for forces/flow, yellow ring highlight, sun icon, red ✗.
- Two full-screen cutaways on dark backgrounds (sand grains, molecule) — the only hard cuts.
- No text labels or number boxes; names of parts are only spoken and captioned. No day/night.

## 5. Captions
- One word at a time (Gemini says 1–3; frames show single words), heavy rounded face, white with thick black outline, centre x, y ≈ 26–28%.
- Punctuation is kept in the caption (commas, «؟», «…»). The incoming word fades up from grey, so some frames show a ghost word. No colour highlights.

## 6. Ending
- 1:07–1:12 molecule aside, 1:13 «باقي تركب البلاط والدرج ولوح القفز» (three parts in 3 s), 1:16 «ومبروك» with confetti, 1:17 «بنيت», 1:18 «بنفسك.» then ~1.5 s hold with no caption. No CTA card.

## 7. Camera and motion
- One locked wide for most of the video; short eased zooms (≈0.5–1 s) to the valve, filter and skimmer, then back.
- Parts wipe/grow in place (mesh, concrete, pipes extend along their route); water level animates; confetti twice.

## 8. What works / weakness
1. Fake «مبروك» at 3 s, then immediate collapse: strongest hook of the five.
2. The "empty pool floats like a boat" beat — a non-obvious problem with a small named fix.
3. Pipes accumulate into a full plumbing diagram the viewer has watched being built.
- Weakness: the villa backdrop never changes and takes the top third, so many beats look alike in a scroll; no on-screen term labels for skimmer/hydrostatic valve.
