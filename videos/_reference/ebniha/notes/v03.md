## 8. Why it works / weakness
1. Left-to-right line means the build order and the process order are the same picture.
2. Colour-coded silos/units make each ingredient memorable without text.
3. Hook is a natural phenomenon, not a human action — strong first frame.
- Weakness: no characters or labels and long near-identical conveyor stretches (33–54 s) feel static.
