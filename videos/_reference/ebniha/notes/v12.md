# v12 — الرافعة البرجية (tower crane) · 116 s

## 1. Topic & look
- Builds a tower crane from "rope over a pulley on a pole".
- Flat 2D vector with black outlines (cartoon), strict side elevation. Palette: sky blue gradient + big soft sun, white outlined clouds, yellow lattice steel, grey concrete frame building, red load, brown soil in 3 bands, pale beige skyline silhouettes.
- Setting: generic construction site, no people at all (cabin is empty; "the operator" is implied). Not culturally specific.

## 2. Opening (0–3 s)
- Frame 0: wide, wooden pole with a pulley wheel at top, rope, red bucket of bricks on the grass; soil cross-section fills the bottom 30 %. Caption «لو».
- A one-storey concrete frame pops up beside it at 0.3 s; bucket is pulled up the pole by 2.5–4 s. Static camera. Hook is purely the action + «كذا انت سويت رافعة».

The crane is one object that never leaves; every fix is added to it and kept.

## 4. Recurring devices
- Soil cross-section always on (3 brown bands), used once for the foundation.
- Tags: dark charcoal pill, yellow text, near the part, ~1.5 s. Problem tags are the same pill with red/salmon text and «!» or ✗. Numbers in the same pill, larger for «72 كم/س».
- Failure is acted out (mast falls, crane leans) before the fix. Day→night→day grade for the fun fact. Wind arrows. Colour-coded jib (green safe → red limit).

## 5. Captions
- One word at a time, bold white with dark outline, centred, y ≈ 72 % (over the soil). No highlight colours. ~3.3 words/s.

## 6. Ending (last 10 s)
- 104–111 anemometer close-up + «72 كم/س»; 112 zoom-out; «مبروك عليك… بنفسك!» lands on the full wide at ~114–116. No CTA card, no vote, no logo; ends on the held wide.

## 7. Camera & motion
- Continuous, mostly locked wide; 4–5 eased zooms into the crane head and back. The frame keeps re-scaling so the growing building + crane still fit (ground line drops lower each time).
- Parts drop from above and settle; lattice sections stack; mound of dirt appears with the pit.

## 8. Works / weakness
1. Each problem is shown failing physically (topple, lean, out of reach) so the fix feels earned.
2. Numbers are attached to the moving load, not floating: 8 t → 1.8 t reads instantly.
3. Night interlude resets attention at 80 % of a long video.
- Weakness: 116 s is long for the format; the middle (55–72) is three beats on the same idea with little new to look at, and tags are small.
