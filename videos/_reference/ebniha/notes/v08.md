# v08 — «بنيت نظام نقل وفرز الأمتعة بنفسك» (airport baggage system) · 110.5 s

## 1. Topic & look
- Builds a **baggage handling system**: cart to the plane → check-in scale + belt through the wall → heavy tag → 10-digit barcode → laser tag reader → CT scanner → manual search → tilt-tray sorter → early-bag rack → tug + belt loader → claim carousel → flight display.
- Flat 2D vector with dark outlines on every shape (more cartoon than v06/07/09/10), 2.5D only for the oval sorter and carousel. Palette: grey-beige concrete interior with grain, sky blue with outlined clouds, bright candy suitcases (pink, teal, yellow, orange, purple), green-tailed Saudi jet.
- Setting: King Abdulaziz airport, Jeddah (sign on the terminal). No people at all; the orange suitcase with a «ثقيلة» sticker is the hero we follow.

## 2. Opening (0–3 s)
- Frame 0: apron side view, a blue cart stacked with pink/green/teal bags at lower left (y ≈ 58 %), jet tail entering from the right, a terminal wall edge on the left. The cart rolls right toward the plane while the camera pans. Caption from 0.3 s. 2.5 s tag «نظام نقل الأمتعة!».

## 4. Recurring devices
- No ground cross-section; a yellow floor line and concrete wall instead.
- Tags: dark pill, cream/yellow text; red text for problems. In-world screens (departures board, CT monitor, claim sign) carry numbers.
- Big counter box for the statistic; magnified barcode card; x-ray "skin off" view of the CT; ✓ on the monitor, «إنذار!» for fail.
- Dashed yellow outline as a size comparison (van).

## 5. Captions
- White heavy sans, dark outline, centred, y ≈ 72 %, one word each. No box, no highlight.

## 6. Ending
- 97.5–102.5: display sign beat. 102.5–104.5: «مبروك عليك، بنيت نظام نقل وفرز الأمتعة بنفسك» while the camera pulls far back from the carousel hall.
- ~104.5: dim; **blue** speech bubble «اكتب فكرتك!» (matches this video's palette), «أكثر لايكات = الفيديو الجاي», four cream vote rows, counters tick, «فكرتك أنت!» wins at 999. No confetti seen. Ends 110.5.

## 7. Camera & motion
- Continuous travelling shot along the belt, left to right and downward, inside → outside → second airport; zooms for details. Bags slide, tilt and drop with weight; machines fade from solid to see-through.

## 8. What works / weakness
- One named real place and one hard number in the first 10 s.
- A single bag as protagonist turns a system into a story.
- The CT beat has three visual stages (size outline, see-through, 3D screen) for one fix.
- Weakness: 110 s and 12 stations; nothing accumulates on screen, so the «بنيت… بنفسك» payoff shot shows only the last station.
