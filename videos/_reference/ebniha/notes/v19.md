# v19 — الدش (shower) · 74 s

## 1. Topic and look
- Builds a shower: bucket on a tree → black roof barrel (solar) → insulated gas water heater → thermostat + relief valve → mixer → pressure-balancing piston → sloped floor, drain and P-trap → shower head and curtain.
- Flat 2D vector with soft gradients; the house is a cut-open 3/4 box (one wall removed) so the bathroom reads like a dollhouse.
- Palette: cream walls, orange tiled roof, light-blue tiles with a blue checker floor, striped orange-brown soil band (lower ~40%), blue = cold pipes, orange = hot pipes, grey chrome fittings.
- Setting: suburban street with picket fence and neighbouring houses (generic, not Gulf-specific). No people at all.

## 2. Opening (0–3 s)
- Frame 0: big tree on the left with a blue-filled bucket hanging from a branch at caption height, house on the right, caption «إذا» (this one opens with «إذا», not «لو»).
- Slow push-in on the bucket; by 3 s it is leaking from the bottom and half empty: «إذا علقت سطل الموية بشجرة وخرمت قاعه صار عندك دش».

## 4. Recurring devices
- Ground cross-section used functionally at the end (trap in the soil), decorative before that.
- Blue/orange pipe colour code with small moving dots for flow; riser colour change shows the scald.
- X-ray window on the mixer (dark panel inside the chrome plate) — the key cutaway.
- One number on an object («49°C» on the thermostat). No tags, ✓/✗, popup cards, weather or night.

## 5. Captions
- One word at a time, same heavy rounded face, white with dark outline, centre x, y ≈ 28%. No highlights. Often lands on top of the object being explained (bucket, tank, mixer), which hides detail.

## 6. Ending
- 1:04–1:09 shower head + curtain; 1:09 «ومبروك», then «بدأت بسطل وانتهيت بدش» over a zoom-out to the first framing (house only, no tree). Holds ~3 s with the curtain closed. No confetti, no CTA card. The sign-off is a callback to the opening prop instead of «بنفسك».

## 7. Camera and motion
- Fully continuous, no hard cuts: pans and zooms follow the water path (tree → roof → tank → wall → mixer → drain).
- Parts fade/pop in; pipes draw along their route; fills change colour; mixer internals slide.

## 8. What works / weakness
1. Follows the water in order, so the camera path is the explanation.
2. The flush-scald problem is something everyone has felt; the piston x-ray pays it off with one moving part.
3. Closing line mirrors the opening («بسطل… بدش»), a tidy loop.
- Weakness: shortest and most rushed; five fixes in the last 20 s, and the caption sits on the subject. The opening bucket is small and low-contrast for a first frame.
