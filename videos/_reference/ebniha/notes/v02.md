# v02 — مترو (metro) · 78 s

## 1. Topic & look
- Builds a driverless metro line: hand-dug tunnel → TBM → lining → rail/power → control → station.
- Full 3D low-poly, same engine/look as v01. The frame is a vertical cut: top ~35 % city street, the rest is
  ground in flat sand/beige with one orange stratum band; the tunnel is a window cut into it.
- Palette: beige/sand earth, orange stratum, blue-striped office blocks, palms, TBM in red/yellow with translucent
  shell showing purple/blue/orange internals, grey concrete, white train with blue stripe.
- Setting: Riyadh (ending shows a Kingdom-Centre-like tower). No people except implied; cars on street.

## 2. Opening (0–3 s)
- Frame 0: street with green and orange cars, palms, blue-striped building; below it an empty rectangular
  cavity in the sand. Caption «حفرت».
- Within 1 s a shovel swings inside the cavity, rail and a small orange cart appear by 2.5 s; camera pushes in.

## 4. Recurring devices
- Ground cross-section is the whole stage (street above, cut below) for the entire video.
- Translucent "x-ray" shell on the TBM instead of a separate cutaway.
- Arrows as the diagram layer: cyan = data/cold, orange = heat. No text labels, no number boxes
  (the 170 km fact is spoken only; trophy + map props stand in).
- No weather/day-night.

## 5. Captions
- One word at a time, heavy white rounded Arabic, soft dark shadow, centred y ≈ 72 %, slightly smaller than v01.
- Pop-in per word; no highlights. Legibility drops on the pale sand background.

## 6. Ending
- 62.5 s pause icon over darkened frame; 64–66 s blue pill «ودّك تسوي فيديوهات زي هاذي؟» + white «الرابط في البايو» card.
- 67–71 s extras shown as three quick close-ups (escalator, ticket machines, map).
- 72–78 s «ومبروك عليك، بنيت مترو بنفسك»: fast pull-back to city skyline over the cut ground, last 3 s
  caption-free hold. No end card.

## 7. Camera & motion
- Mostly continuous tracking left/right along the tunnel with push-ins; 3–4 real cuts for close-ups (cutter head,
  cab, extras). Medium-fast, always moving.
- Parts are lowered in, slide along the bore, or click into place (segments); arrows flow in loops.

## 8. Why it works / weakness
1. Street-over-tunnel framing keeps "why" (traffic) and "how" in one picture.
2. The spinning cutter close-up at 12 s is a strong retention spike right after the first «عشان كذا».
3. Local pride fact (Riyadh) placed just before the CTA.
- Weakness: beige-on-beige; large empty sand areas and low caption contrast; no labels for TBM parts.
