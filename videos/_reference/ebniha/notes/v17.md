## 4. Recurring devices
- No ground cross-section here: the "cut" is the opened shaft; only a thin road/soil strip at the bottom of wides.
- Floor-number tiles as a constant scale reference; controller display shows the current floor.
- Two-level staging: roof cutaways (motor, governor) vs shaft views.
- No text labels, tags, ✓/✗ or popup cards at all; danger shown by a red spark and smoke only. No day/night, no weather.

## 5. Captions
- One word at a time, same heavy rounded face, white with thick dark outline, centre x, y ≈ 28%. No colour highlights. In wides the word sits right on the building, which hurts contrast.

## 6. Ending
- 1:14–1:17: buttons/bell/light close-up. 1:17 «ومبروك», 1:18 «بنيت» while the camera pulls out; 1:19–1:22 silent hold on the full building with the cabin riding, no caption, no confetti, no CTA card.

## 7. Camera and motion
- Constant vertical travel: the camera rides with the cabin, so floors scroll past; alternates close (cabin) and wide (whole building) about every 5 s.
- Mostly continuous zooms; hard cuts only to the roof machinery. Parts fade in or draw along their length (shaft, rails); the brake is a snap.

## 8. What works / weakness
1. The tall building fits 9:16 perfectly; vertical motion keeps every shot alive.
2. The two riders make every failure personal (swing, free-fall) without any dialogue.
3. Close/wide rhythm: each fix is shown as a mechanism up close, then proven in the wide.
- Weakness: zero on-screen labels or numbers, so terms like governor/safety brake live only in the audio; the ending is flat (no confetti, last word cut before «بنفسك» is shown).
