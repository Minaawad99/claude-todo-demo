# v10 — «بنيت شاحنة خلاطة خرسانة بنفسك» (concrete mixer truck) · 75.8 s

## 1. Topic & look
- Builds a **concrete mixer truck**: bucket mix → factory batch in a dump truck → rotating drum with hopper and hydraulic motor → tilted drum → spiral blade (in/out) → chute → extra lift axle → 90-minute clock → wash-water tank.
- Flat 2D vector with gradients and gritty textures; side view. Palette: beige city blocks, blue sky with hazy sun, yellow tower crane and dump truck, orange-and-white striped drum, dark asphalt, red-brown soil strata.
- Setting: Gulf construction site and city road. Characters: worker in hard hat and orange hi-vis vest; truck driver in the cab.

## 2. Opening (0–3 s)
- Frame 0: worker at left bent over a white bucket on gravel in front of a block wall with a dark opening; soil section with rocks below (ground at ~58 %). He empties a cement bag, shovels sand, pours water, stirs. Caption from 0.2 s. At 3.5 s tag «خرسانة بدائية!».

## 4. Recurring devices
- Ground cross-section in every shot: gravel, asphalt, sub-base, red soil with rocks, an orange and a blue line, a dark pile; cracks are shown in it.
- Tags: dark pill, cream/yellow text. Running counter tag for the bucket number; scale readout box for tonnage; clock badges for time.
- **See-through skin** toggles on the tipper bed and the drum whenever the inside matters.
- One cream comparison card. No ✓/✗. Daylight only; out-of-focus palm leaf in the foreground for depth.

## 5. Captions
- White heavy sans, dark outline, centred, y ≈ 72 % (on the road/soil), one word each. No highlight.

## 6. Ending
- 65–68.5: wash tank. 68.5–70.5: «ومبروك عليك بنيت شاحنة خلاطة خرسانة بنفسك!», whole truck centred, confetti, camera drifts back.
- ~70: dim; orange bubble «أنت تقرر!», «اكتب فكرتك وخلّها تجمع لايكات», three then four vote rows, «فكرتك أنت!» reaches 999 and the rest fade. Ends 75.8.

## 7. Camera & motion
- Continuous except one blurred whip-pan (7.5 s) that acts as a cut. Truck drives through a scrolling city; frequent push-in to the drum and pull-back to the whole truck. Parts swap by cross-fade on the chassis; wheels drop; drum rotation never stops.

## 8. What works / weakness
- The counter racing to 300 buckets makes the first problem felt in 3 s.
- The cutaway drum explains the spiral in two arrows (in, out).
- Man with a jackhammer inside the drum: a memorable consequence image.
- Weakness: «وش الغريب» card arrives late (52 s) and its two-panel diagram is abstract compared with the rest.
