# v07 — «بنيت حمام طيارة بنفسك» (aircraft toilet) · 93.1 s

## 1. Topic & look
- Builds an **airliner lavatory**: bucket behind a curtain → seat on sealed tank with lid → recirculating blue fluid → vacuum toilet with one rear tank → ground fan → non-stick bowl + rinse ring → heated sink drain → service truck → lock, smoke sensor, ashtray.
- Flat 2D vector with gradients; side cutaway of a fuselage. Palette: white cabin, navy seats and curtain, gold/yellow fuselage frame band, beige cargo hold with multicoloured containers, blue sky, white cloud floor; green Saudia-style tail stripe.
- Setting: in flight over clouds, later on the apron. Characters: thobe + shemagh passenger, woman in black abaya, steward in navy uniform.

## 2. Opening (0–3 s)
- Frame 0: cabin cutaway mid-height (floor at ~62 %), cargo hold below, clouds at the bottom. Steward carries a green bucket to the back, sets it down, pulls the blue curtain across, walks away. Caption from 0.2 s («لو حطيت سطل…»). At 2.5 s tag «حمام بدائي!».

## 4. Recurring devices
- No soil section; the "underground" is the cargo hold with the tank, always visible below the floor line.
- Tags as dark pills with cream text; problem tags use red/pink text.
- Instrument: round black altimeter with a readout under it, top-right, returns three times.
- Flow shown as dotted lines with small yellow chevrons; liquid levels animate in the tank.
- Two cream cards (cistern maths, law). No ✓/✗ except ✓ next to the altitude.
- Weather/place change instead of day/night: clouds ↔ apron.

## 5. Captions
- Same heavy white sans with dark outline, centred, y ≈ 72 %, one word at a time. No highlights.

## 6. Ending
- 85.4–87.5: «ومبروك عليك، بنيت حمام طيارة بنفسك!» on a wide shot of the tail on the apron with the truck; confetti.
- ~87.5: dim + blur, orange bubble «عندك فكرة؟», line «اكتبها تحت، والأكثر لايكات يصير فيديو», the same four vote rows on cream cards; counts tick; at ~91 s a yellow ribbon «الفيديو الجاي!» lands on «فكرتك أنت!» (999). Ends 93.1.

## 7. Camera & motion
- Continuous; constant slow zoom/pan between close bowl views and wide fuselage views; the only big vertical move is the ice fall. Parts drop in or draw on; fluids and air are the main animation.

## 8. What works / weakness
- A comic first frame (bucket on a plane) that states the topic without a word of title.
- Invisible physics (pressure difference) is made visible with dots, arrows and the altimeter.
- Two surprising facts (blue ice, ashtray required by law) are spaced at 28 s and 80 s.
- Weakness: the longest in the set with 9 fixes; the middle (50–75 s) is a string of small tags on the same framing.
