# v05 — صاروخ (rocket) · 76 s

## 1. Topic & look
- Builds an orbital rocket from a gunpowder tube.
- Flat 2D vector with grain, bold dark outlines, same family as v04 but richer. Rocket drawn as a cutaway
  (navy shell, amber fuel, pale-blue LOX, visible pumps/chamber). Ground cross-section brown at the bottom ~30 %.
- Palette: warm sand/beige desert, hazy palms, navy + amber + sky-blue rocket, orange flame, grey steel stand.
- Setting: Gulf desert launch site. Characters: man in white thobe + red shemagh, engineer in orange suit/white helmet.

## 2. Opening (0–3 s)
- Frame 0: man in thobe pouring powder from a scoop into a short black tube on a stick, desert behind; caption «لو».
- ~2 s he bends and lights the fuse (spark); by 3 s the tube shoots off and tumbles; a stopwatch icon with
  «ثواني بس!» follows at ~5 s.

## 4. Recurring devices
- Tags: black pill + yellow text beside the part; red text variants for danger.
- Round icon badges (drop, stopwatch, thermometer, speaker, no-air) with a short yellow outlined phrase under them.
- Popup cards (cream frame, title on top, mini-animation inside, dimmed background) — 5 of them; multi-state.
- Numbers shown big inside cards («٨٠٪» with bar, «٧٫٨ كم/ث»); ⚠ triangle; crossed-circle for "no".
- Ground cross-section present but unused (decor only). No weather/day-night (space shown inside cards).

## 5. Captions
- One word at a time, heavy rounded white Arabic, black outline, centred y ≈ 72 %; each word pops from ~70 % to
  full in 1–2 frames and holds until the next. No highlight colour (yellow is reserved for tags).

## 6. Ending
- 65–68 s site completes. 68–70 s «ومبروك عليك! بنيت صاروخ بنفسك!» as the rocket lifts off with smoke + confetti.
- 70–76 s dim overlay, orange bubble «صوّت بلايك!», sub-line «أكثر فكرة عليها لايكات تفوز», four rows (برج مراقبة,
  فكرتك أنت!, جسر معلّق, عجلة دوّارة) with heart counters ticking from 0; «فكرتك أنت!» highlighted; hearts float.

## 7. Camera & motion
- One continuous 2D camera: tilt up/down the rocket, push in to the engine, pull back for context; cards replace
  cuts. Brisk — a new element roughly every 1.5–2 s.
- Parts slide/fade in, fills rise, gears spin, tags pop with overshoot, cards scale in and out.

## 8. Why it works / weakness
1. Cutaway rocket means every fix is a visible organ added to the same body.
2. Cards carry the abstract ideas (oxygen, staging, orbit) without leaving the scene.
3. Densest labelling of the set; fully followable muted, and the thobe character gives scale and humour.
- Weakness: card-heavy second half (46–65 s) hides the build for long stretches; many tags flash too briefly.
