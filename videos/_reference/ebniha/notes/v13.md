# v13 — درج كهربائي (escalator) · 74.8 s

## 1. Topic & look
- Turns a fixed staircase into an escalator.
- 2.5D isometric-ish vector illustration (stairs drawn in 3/4 view on a flat backdrop), textured/"printed" finish with diagonal light streaks. Palette: teal/sea-green mall, cream floor tiles, steel-blue steps with yellow edge lines, orange motor, green potted plants framing the bottom corners.
- Setting: shopping-mall atrium (shop windows with mannequins, glass roof). No characters; narrator uses «نبدأ / نركب» (we) rather than «تركب».

## 2. Opening (0–3 s)
- Frame 0: finished ordinary staircase between two floors, centred; caption «لو». Static for 3 s while the line «…باقي عليك تطلعه برجولك» plays.
- 3.5–5 s: push-in on the steps with the question «كيف يطلعك وأنت واقف؟», then the staircase ghosts out (fades to 20 %) leaving ONE step floating. Hook = a question, not a claim.

## 4. Recurring devices
- No ground cross-section. The equivalent is x-ray / ghosting: outer parts go semi-transparent to show chains, return run and machine room.
- Almost no tags: only one on-art word («مستوية»). Parts are identified by the caption word alone.
- Red = problem (dashed tilt outline, brake highlight); yellow = safety edges/comb. No numbers, no ✓/✗, no cards, no day/night.

## 5. Captions
- One word at a time, bold white with dark soft shadow, centred, y ≈ 72 % (on the floor tiles between the plants). Punctuation kept («واقف؟», «باستمرار…»). ~3 words/s, the calmest pacing of the five.

## 6. Ending (last 10 s)
- 64–67 casing on; 67–71 last x-ray of the loop; 71.5 «مبروك… بنفسك» over the complete escalator, steps still moving. No CTA, no logo. Ends at 74.8 s on the moving loop (loopable).

## 7. Camera & motion
- Continuous; gentle eased zooms (in for a part, out for context), never a cut. Slow compared with v11/v12.
- Parts fade/slide in along the incline; duplication of the step is a ripple up the slope; mechanisms loop continuously once added.

## 8. Works / weakness
1. Starts from ONE step and multiplies it: the mechanism is understood before the machine exists.
2. Ghosted x-ray instead of a cutaway keeps scale and position constant.
3. Final "it's the same steps going round" reveal gives a payoff beyond «مبروك».
- Weakness: no labels or numbers, so part names live only in audio/captions; the busy teal background lowers contrast with the blue-grey steps.
