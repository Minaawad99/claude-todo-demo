# v09 — «بنيت آلة آيسكريم سوفت بنفسك» (soft-serve machine) · 83.8 s

## 1. Topic & look
- Builds a **soft-serve machine**: bowl in the freezer → can in ice + salt with hand crank and dasher → motor → refrigerant coil + compressor (freezing cylinder) → cold hopper on top → motor-load sensing → draw valve + star nozzle → nightly heat-treat cycle → lockout → drip tray and cone.
- Flat 2D vector with gradients and brushed-metal textures; front cutaway of the machine. Palette: sand-coloured Najdi mud-brick walls and minaret silhouette, blue sky with hazy sun, steel greys, copper-orange coil, cream mix, dark control screen with green text.
- Setting: outdoor Saudi old-town street, wooden table then pavement. No full character: a white thobe sleeve and hand enters from the top corner to pour, crank and pull.

## 2. Opening (0–3 s)
- Frame 0: white bowl with blue rings on a wooden table (table top at y ≈ 58 %), small glass freezer «-١٨°» to the right; a white-sleeved arm from the top-left pours a milk carton «حليب», then cream, then sugar. 2.2 s the bowl slides into the freezer. Caption from 0.2 s. Tag «آيسكريم بدائي» follows.

## 7. Camera & motion
- Continuous, mostly locked on the machine with slow push-ins and pull-backs; less travel than the others. Parts drop from above or fade/morph in place (ice → coil, table → cabinet). The hand gives every step a human action.

## 8. What works / weakness
- Starts in a kitchen with things everyone owns, ends on a machine everyone has seen.
- The "why is it always broken" beat answers a question the viewer already has.
- Machine screen doubles as caption for state, so few floating tags are needed.
- Weakness: 36–45 s (amp sensing) is three near-identical frames with only a number changing.
