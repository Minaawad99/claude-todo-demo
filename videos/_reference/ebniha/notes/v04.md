# v04 — سد (dam) · 70 s

## 1. Topic & look
- Builds a concrete hydro dam from a pile of rocks in a river.
- Flat 2D vector, paper/grain texture, thin dark outlines. Side cross-section: sky + brown hills (little green
  triangles as bushes) on top, blue river line, sandy bed, then grey bedrock filling the bottom ~35 %.
- Palette: muted earth browns, slate grey rock, one saturated blue (water), yellow crane/excavator, red accents.
- Setting: generic wadi between mountains; a pale city skyline appears at the end. No people.

## 2. Opening (0–3 s)
- Frame 0: tight on the river surface, brown hillside behind, grey rock below; caption «لو».
- Rocks fall from the top of frame one after another and splash into a pile (white foam puffs); at ~2.7 s a
  black tag «سد بدائي» pops above the pile.

## 3. Build beats
| t | narration | on screen | camera |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3.5 | لو: pile rocks in a river = primitive dam | falling rocks, tag «سد بدائي» | static close |
| 3.5–7 | بس: water pushes through, first flood removes it | pile washes away, red tag «فيضان!» | pull back to show hills |
| 7–9.5 | عشان كذا: concrete | white dashed ghost outline of dam + tag «سد خرسانة» | hold |
| 9.5–16 | لكن can't pour in a running river → diversion tunnel | tower crane arm + hook enters top; dark tube under bed tagged «نفق تحويل» | slow push |
| 16–19 | cofferdams + pumps | two trapezoid earth banks, tag «مضخة», bed turns dry cream | hold |
| 19–27 | بس: cracks leak → dig to hard rock, grout | black cracks + dashed seep line, red tag «شقوق»; excavator, tag «صخر صلب», «حقن إسمنت», white grout blobs fill cracks | tilt down slightly |
| 27–30 | pour block by block | grey blocks stack in a staircase, tag «بلوك ورا بلوك» | hold |
| 30–36 | بس: mass concrete heats/cracks → cooling pipes | blocks glow orange, tag «حرارة!»; blue serpentine pipes draw in, tag «أنابيب تبريد» | push in |
| 36–43 | لكن no power → penstock, turbine, generator | dark diagonal pipe, powerhouse box with spinning turbine; tags «توربين», «مولّد» | pan right/down to toe of dam |
| 43–50 | بس flood overtops → spillway | popup card (cream, rounded, shadow) mini-diagram with ⚠ and footer «الموية تطوف فوق السد!»; then chute tagged «مفيض» | background dims under card; pull back |
| 50–57 | الغريب: arch shape sends pressure to the valley walls | popup card, top-down view, «البحيرة», arrows, orange arch highlight, footer «الضغط يروح للجبال» | static card |
| 57–62 | باقي: close tunnels, lake fills, power lines | water rises behind dam, tag «بحيرة», pylons to city | wide pull-back |
| 62–70 | مبروك + poll | confetti; poll card | hold |
- Persistent: crane arm, grout curtain, cofferdams, pipes all remain; dam stays centre from 27 s.

## 4. Recurring devices
- Tags: small black rounded pill, yellow bold text, pointer nub, placed just above the part; red text for
  problems («شقوق», «فيضان!», «حرارة!»). 1–3 words, on for ~2 s.
- Dashed ghost outline to preview what will be built.
- Popup cards for side explanations (2 of them), with ⚠ triangle in the corner; scene behind darkens ~40 %.
- No numbers, no ✓/✗, no day/night.

## 5. Captions
- One word at a time, heavy rounded white Arabic with black outline, centred y ≈ 72 % on the grey rock area
  (excellent contrast). Pop-in scale; no colour highlights; «!» kept on emphatic words.

## 6. Ending
- 57–62 s wide shot, lake + city + power lines. 62.5 s «ومبروك عليك! بنيت سد بنفسك!» with a confetti burst.
- 64–70 s scene dims; orange speech-bubble header «أنت تقرر!», sub-line «اكتب فكرتك وخلّها تجمع لايكات», then
  four white option rows pop in one by one (صاروخ, فكرتك أنت!, جسر معلّق, عجلة دوّارة) each with a heart
  like-counter that counts up; «فكرتك أنت!» row enlarges/highlights; pink hearts float up. No bio/pause CTA.

## 7. Camera & motion
- One continuous shot; only slow push/pull and small pans (2D parallax of hills). Much calmer than the 3D ones.
- Parts fade/slide/stack in; lines draw on (cracks, pipes); tags and cards pop with overshoot.

## 8. Why it works / weakness
1. Labels on every new part make it understandable muted.
2. Red problem tags vs yellow fix tags mirror the «بس / عشان كذا» rhythm visually.
3. Poll ending turns the series format into a comment prompt.
- Weakness: bottom third is empty grey rock for most of the video; drawing is small in frame in wide shots.
