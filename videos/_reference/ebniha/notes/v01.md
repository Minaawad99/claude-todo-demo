# v01 — برج اتصالات (cell tower) · 90 s

## 1. Topic & look
- Builds a cellular tower, from "wire on a stick" to a tower disguised as a palm.
- Full 3D low-poly (Blender-like), soft shadows, photographic cloud sky. Camera sits at ground level so the
  bottom ~25–30 % of frame is a dark brown textured soil cross-section (the caption lives on it).
- Palette: grass green lawn with mown stripes, cream/grey box houses with blue windows, red-and-white lattice
  tower, red pickup, tan boulders, brown soil. Signals = translucent mint/cyan arcs; warning signal = salmon red.
- Setting: Saudi/Gulf low-rise neighbourhood with date palms; later a sand "البر" patch. Character: tiny man in
  white thobe + red shemagh, also riding the red pickup.

## 2. Opening (0–3 s)
- Frame 0: street-level side view, house left, palm centre, man in thobe at the kerb, red pickup entering right,
  soil band below. Caption «ركبت» already on. No title card, no logo.
- Hook is acted: camera pushes in on the man, a stick with a wire rises beside him (≈1.5 s), by 3 s mint arcs
  radiate from it to a friend's dish on the pickup.

## 3. Build beats
| t | narration | on screen | camera |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–6 | لو: wire on a stick + radio → talk to the next حارة | man, stick, mint arcs, pickup with dish | push-in, then track right with pickup |
| 6–11 | بس: signal fades, houses/hills block it | pickup drives behind house, then behind boulder; arcs stop | track right |
| 11–17 | عشان كذا: steel tower >30 m, triangle bracing | concrete pad, lattice segments drop from top of frame and stack; bracing flashes yellow | crane up + pull back to reveal town |
| 17–23 | بس: one tower can't cover city / jams | high aerial, cyan dome over one tower | fly up to bird's-eye |
| 23–28 | فـ: split city into cells | yellow hexagon lines draw on ground, a tower pops in each cell | slow aerial drift |
| 28–38 | لكن: round antenna wastes signal → 3 flat panels | rings pulsing up/down around mast; 3 panels, cyan sector beams | orbit close on tower top |
| 38–48 | بعدين: link to next tower → dish / fibre | dish + thin beam between towers; orange cable drawn in soil | drop to ground level, soil band returns |
| 48–55 | cabinet: radios, batteries, AC | grey cabinet door open, shelves, AC fan; cable from tower | close-up at base |
| 55–62 | لكن: lightning → rod + earth wire | dark cloud slides in, scene dims, bolt; rod on top, wire to ground | medium, tilt up |
| 62–75 | وتدري وش الغريب: phone shouts louder far away; handover | man on sand holding phone, arc turns red; phone close-up; pickup crossing cells | cut-like whip to sand, then wide |
| 75–80 | CTA | pause icon, link-in-bio cards | hold |

- Persistent: tower stays centre from 11 s to the end; every added part (panels, dish, cabinet, rod) remains.

## 4. Recurring devices
- Soil cross-section whenever camera is at ground level (fibre cable and earth wire drawn inside it).
- No text labels or number boxes at all — parts are explained by narration only.
- Signal arcs/rings/beams as the only "diagram" layer; colour = state (mint ok, red weak).
- Weather beat: storm cloud + global dimming for lightning. No day/night.

## 5. Captions
- One word at a time, heavy rounded Arabic sans, white with soft dark outline/shadow, centred at y ≈ 72 %.
- Each word pops (scales up from ~70 %) on its syllable; no colour highlights.

## 6. Ending
- 75.0 s: screen darkens slightly, big white circular pause icon centre (≈1.5 s) on «بس قبل لا تشغله».
- 77–80 s: blue pill «ودّك تسوي فيديوهات زي هاذي؟» (y≈8 %) + white card «الرابط في البايو» (y≈14 %).
- 80–86 s: finishing touches; 86–90 s «ومبروك عليك بنيت برج اتصالات بنفسك» over palm-tower hero, plane passes,
  camera pulls back to town with soil band. No end card.

## 7. Camera & motion
- One continuous flying camera (ground → crane → aerial → orbit → ground); essentially no hard cuts except the
  jump to the sand scene at 62 s. Always drifting, never static.
- Parts drop in from above and stack, or pop with a small overshoot; lines draw on.

## 8. Why it works / weakness
1. The object stays centre for 80 s and visibly accumulates parts.
2. Scale change (street → city hexagons → back) gives each problem its own vantage.
3. Joke payoff (tower dressed as palm) lands the مبروك.
- Weakness: no labels, so with sound off the technical terms are lost; aerial shots get busy and tiny.
