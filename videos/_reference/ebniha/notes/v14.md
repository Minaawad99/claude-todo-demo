# v14 — شاحنة زبالة (garbage truck) · 67.2 s

## 1. Topic & look
- Builds a side-loader compactor truck from "open pickup bed + rubbish".
- Flat 2D cartoon vector with thick dark outlines, side view. Palette: saturated pastel street (mint, terracotta, ochre houses), grey steel box with green stripe, white cab, yellow hydraulic parts, green wheelie bin, black/green bags, grey road with yellow-black kerb, beige paving in front.
- Setting: Arab old-town street (arched windows, mashrabiya, rooftop dishes); landfill at the end. Tiny driver visible only at the end. No ground cross-section until the landfill.

## 2. Opening (0–3 s)
- Frame 0: small flatbed truck (wooden slat sides, white cab) parked in the street; a black bin bag is already falling from the top of frame. Caption «لو».
- Bags keep dropping into the bed one per ~0.3 s while the camera pushes in; at ~2.5 s a big yellow title word «شاحنة زبالة؟» sits over the truck. Hook is the joke claim + question mark.

## 4. Recurring devices
- X-ray of the box with a dimmed/vignetted world ("spotlight") for inside beats; back to full colour for outside beats.
- On-art words are large display type without a pill: yellow or white with dark shadow («شاحنة زبالة؟», «هوا!», «1/5», «أريزونا 1969»); part names sometimes dim grey-white.
- Arrow + dashed circle for the clearance rule. Stink lines. One fraction, one count, one date. No ✓/✗, no cards.
- Brown soil cross-section appears only in the landfill scene.

## 5. Captions
- One word at a time, bold white with dark outline, centred, y ≈ 72 % (on the kerb line). Separate from the yellow display words higher up. ~3.5 words/s.

## 6. Ending (last 10 s)
- 57–61 trivia text over the landfill; 61.5 beeper/camera; 64.5 «مبروك، بنيت شاحنة زبالة» (no «بنفسك» here). No CTA or vote card. Ends on the truck at the landfill.

## 7. Camera & motion
- Continuous except one real scene change (street → landfill at ~50 s). Constant small pushes/pulls; nothing held longer than ~3 s.
- Parts drop in from above; compaction is a horizontal wipe; arm animates as a rigid 2-joint swing.

## 8. Works / weakness
1. Dimmed x-ray mode makes "inside the box" unmistakable without leaving the shot.
2. Explains a real-life rule the viewer knows (bin clearance) from the mechanism.
3. Funny, honest hook image: bags literally thrown at a pickup.
- Weakness: the display words overlap each other and the art in places («غودزيلا» over «أريزونا 1969»), and the ending has no payoff shot of the truck working or driving away.
