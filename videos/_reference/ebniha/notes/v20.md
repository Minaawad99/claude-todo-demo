# v20 — طريق الأسفلت (asphalt road) · 77 s

## 1. Topic and look
- Builds a road: worn track → dirt road → stone base → camber + side ditches → asphalt → crack sealing → lines, reflectors, speed sign.
- Real 3D render (low-poly, game-engine look): one-point perspective straight down the road, with the front face of the ground cut away as a textured soil cross-section (lower ~40%).
- Palette: striped bright green field, red-brown rocky soil, white/grey stones, charcoal asphalt, photo-like blue sky with cumulus clouds, white houses with red roofs, olive-like trees.
- Setting: Mediterranean/European-looking countryside rather than Gulf. Characters: none; a dark-red boxy SUV is the recurring actor, plus one white truck.

## 2. Opening (0–3 s)
- Frame 0: empty green field to the horizon, soil section below, no caption for the first ~0.5 s, then «لو صرت».
- A tiny SUV appears at the vanishing point and drives toward camera; under it the grass fades into a tan dirt strip («العشب يختفي ويصير عندك طريق ترابي», done by 5 s).

## 4. Recurring devices
- Ground cross-section is the hero: every fix adds a layer to it.
- Weather and time as problem triggers: rain (0:05), winter night (0:40), dusk for reflectors.
- Arrows (red ←→ expansion, red ↓ load), yellow load cones, green ✓ box, one white equation card with emoji and a number.
- Macro cutaway into the asphalt for the freeze-thaw cycle. No part labels.

## 5. Captions
- One word at a time (Gemini: 1–2), heavy rounded face, white with thick black outline, centre x, y ≈ 28%. No highlights. Reads cleanly because it always sits on sky.

## 6. Ending
- 1:01–1:09 truck fact with the equation card (the "did you know" slot, right before the finish).
- 1:09–1:14 lines, reflectors, sign in 5 s; 1:14 «ومبروك عليك» with confetti from the top, 1:16 «بنفسك» as the SUV drives up; ends ~1 s later. No CTA card.

## 7. Camera and motion
- One locked hero angle for ~70% of the runtime; hard cuts only to macro/night shots and back. Almost no camera drift.
- Motion comes from the car driving toward camera, falling stones, layers sweeping toward the horizon and lighting changes.

## 8. What works / weakness
1. A single fixed frame where the section literally stacks up — the clearest "build" of the five.
2. The car returns after every fix as the test, so each layer is proven, not just shown.
3. Freeze-thaw macro and the «1000 ×» card give two shareable facts with visuals that need no reading.
- Weakness: crude 3D (boxy car, repeating textures) and a static camera make the middle feel slow; the first half-second is an empty field with no caption.
