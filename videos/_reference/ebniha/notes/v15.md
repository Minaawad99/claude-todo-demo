# v15 — عمود كهرباء (utility pole) · 66.5 s

## 1. Topic & look
- Builds a distribution pole from "wooden stick + one wire".
- Real 3D render (low-poly/game-engine look, textured soil, soft shadows, volumetric clouds); the only 3D one of this batch.
- Palette: striped bright-green lawn, grey road, cream houses with orange roofs and navy shutters, brown wood pole, dark brown soil, grey transformer.
- Setting: US-style suburb (identical two-storey houses, picket fences). No people. One bird.

## 2. Opening (0–3 s)
- Frame 0: the pole is already mid-drop from the top of frame into a square cut of turf that exposes soil; lands by 0.3 s. Caption «تغرز» (the «لو» is gone in <0.2 s).
- 1.8 s: a single wire draws across the top. 3 s: «صار عندك عمود كهرباء». Static camera, no title.

## 4. Recurring devices
- Ground cross-section as a literal 3D pit cut out of the lawn (not a 2D band); reused for concrete, guy anchor and earthing.
- Part labels: small white outlined text, no box, floating just above/beside the part, appear when «هذه اسمها…» is said.
- Numbers as big coloured outlined text right under the caption: red = danger (13,800 V), green = safe (220 V). No ✓/✗, no cards, no icons. Transformer has a cartoon "face" decal.
- Daylight only; wind streaks once.

## 5. Captions
- One word at a time but LARGE (≈ 2× the others), heavy rounded white with thick black outline, centred, placed HIGH at y ≈ 25 % (in the sky). Words scale-pop in (first frame smaller). Numbers in Eastern Arabic digits. ~3.8 words/s, fastest of the five.

## 6. Ending (last 10 s)
- 57–61 termite fact at the pole base; 61 lamp + ID tag; 63 «مبروك بنيت عمود كهرباء» (no «بنفسك») on a wide of the finished pole. No CTA, no vote card. Hard stop at 66.5 s.

## 7. Camera & motion
- True 3D camera: continuous fly-through with cranes up/down the pole, pushes to parts, pull-backs to the street; a couple of jump-like repositionings but no scene change. Moves every 2–3 s.
- Parts pop/scale in; wires draw along their length; soil breaks into chunks.

## 8. Works / weakness
1. Vertical subject fits 9:16: the camera just rides up and down the pole.
2. Red vs green voltage numbers tell the transformer's job without reading.
3. Everyday "why" answers (bird, meter, termites) packed into the last third keep retention.
- Weakness: US suburb + 3D asset look feels generic and off-brand next to the Gulf-set 2D episodes; captions in the sky pull the eye away from parts added at ground level.
