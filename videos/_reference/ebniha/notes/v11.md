# v11 — مغسلة سيارات (automatic car wash) · 77.9 s

## 1. Topic & look
- Builds a tunnel car wash from "bucket + sponge".
- Flat 2D vector, side elevation, soft gradients and a little depth-of-field blur on fast pans. Not 3D.
- Palette: sand/beige walls, grey tile tunnel, navy car, brown soil band, blue pipes + yellow/orange cables underground, cyan/magenta thin pipes along the tunnel roof, hazy sun in a blue sky.
- Setting: Saudi/Gulf street (beige crenellated walls, palm, green gate). Character: man in white thobe + red shemagh, only in the hook. Second (red) car at the end.

## 2. Opening (0–3 s)
- Frame 0: tight on the man beside the navy car's front wheel, garage shutter, wall clock, blue bucket; ground cross-section already visible in the bottom 25 %. Caption «لو».
- He dips the sponge and scrubs the wheel (suds appear) while camera slowly pulls back to show the whole car by 2.5 s. No title card, no logo: action starts on frame 1. The clock is the visual seed for the first problem «تأخذ ساعة».

## 4. Recurring devices
- Ground cross-section: permanent bottom ~25–30 % (brown soil, pebbles, blue water pipe, orange cable, concrete footings); used for drain and recycling tanks.
- Tags: small dark-charcoal rounded pill, yellow/cream bold text, sits beside the part with a tiny dot/leader; pops in and fades after ~1.5 s.
- Circular zoom bubbles (white ring, pointer to the car) for scratch and salt-spot close-ups. Red ✗ badge and "no brush" circle. One number card (jugs). No ✓.
- Day only; no weather.

## 5. Captions
- One word at a time (occasionally two), bold rounded Arabic sans, white with dark outline/shadow, centred, y ≈ 72 % (on the pavement/soil line). ~3–4 words/s. No colour highlights; emphasis comes from «!» in the word.

## 6. Ending (last 10 s)
- 68–70 neutral sign; 70–73 «ومبروك عليك! بنيت مغسلة سيارات بنفسك» on wide shot.
- 73 s: scene dims (dark overlay), orange speech-bubble card «اكتب فكرتك!» pops at top, sub-line «أكثر لايكات = الفيديو الجاي»; four white vote rows slide in (صاروخ / فكرتك أنت! / جسر معلّق / عجلة دوّارة) each with an icon and a heart counter that ticks up from 0; hearts float up. Holds to 77.9 s.

## 7. Camera & motion
- One continuous take, no hard cuts. Rhythm = hold ~2–4 s on a station, then a quick eased pan to the next (blur on the fast ones). Vertical tilt into the ground twice.
- Parts drop/slide in from above or rise from the ground with a small overshoot; tags pop-scale; foam and water are particle-like loops.

## 8. Works / weakness
1. The car itself is the progress bar: dirty → foamed → rinsed → dry, left to right.
2. Two "surprise fact" bubbles (salt spots, 4 of 5 gallons) break the بس/عشان كذا rhythm.
3. Always-visible soil strip pays off late (recycling tanks) instead of being decoration.
- Weakness: tags are tiny and vanish fast; several pans are blurred enough that a new part is hard to read on first view.
