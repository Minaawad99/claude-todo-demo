# v06 — «بنيت دولة بنفسك» (build a state) · 64.9 s

## 1. Topic & look
- Builds a **country**: tent → border posts + gate → laws + court → police + ministries → roads/water/power → central bank + currency → embassy + passport → UN recognition → flag, anthem, airport.
- Flat 2D vector, side-on, soft paper grain and slight vignette. Palette: sand/beige dunes, pale blue sky, brown soil strata; accents red (tent panel, border posts), teal-green (dome, passport, banknote), blue (water tank, pipes).
- Setting: empty Gulf desert with palms that fills up into a small town. Characters: man in white thobe + red shemagh (the "you"), an intruder in black, women in black abayas, more thobe men as the population grows.

## 2. Opening (0–3 s)
- Frame 0: bare desert, man in thobe at left holding a mallet; ground line at ~67 % with soil cross-section under it. No title card.
- 0.5–1.5 s he hammers and the black goat-hair tent (red panel, yellow triangles) grows up from the ground; 2 s he walks to the right, a campfire and dallah pop in; 2.5 s label «دولة بدائية!». Captions start at 0.3 s («نصبت»).

## 4. Recurring devices
- Ground cross-section in every shot (bottom third): layered brown soil with pebbles; the blue water pipe and black cable appear in it at 26 s and stay.
- Tags: dark rounded pill, cream/yellow bold text, small tail pointing down, placed just above the object; fade after ~2 s.
- Big object cards (banknote, passport) float above the scene, then shrink into it.
- Popup cards: cream rounded card with dark border on a dimmed scene (flags, UN seats); green ✓ per flag; one red ✗ circle (rejected trade).
- No day/night, no weather, no numbers besides «١٠٠» on the note.

## 5. Captions
- Heavy rounded Arabic sans, white with dark outline/soft shadow, no box, centred at y ≈ 72 %, on the soil band.
- One word at a time (occasionally two), swapped on the beat; `!`/`؟` kept. No colour highlight.

## 6. Ending
- 54.5–57: three fast add-ons. 57–59.5: «ومبروك عليك! بنيت دولة بنفسك!» over a wide pull-back of the whole town, confetti falls.
- ~59.5: scene dims and blurs; orange speech bubble «اكتب فكرتك!» at y ≈ 26 %, yellow line «أكثر لايكات = الفيديو الجاي», four vote rows fade in one by one (صاروخ, فكرتك أنت!, جسر معلّق, عجلة دوّارة) with heart counters ticking up; «فكرتك أنت!» wins at 999 and the others fade. Hearts float up. Ends 64.9. No pause icon, no "link in bio".

## 7. Camera & motion
- One continuous side-scroll, no hard cuts; pan plus slow zoom, eased, a new framing every 2–4 s. Motion blur on the fast pans.
- Parts grow up from the ground or drop in with a small overshoot; people walk in; tags pop.

## 8. What works / weakness
- An abstract idea (statehood) is turned into physical objects you can drop on a street.
- The single growing strip makes the final pull-back a real payoff.
- The «وش الغريب» card breaks the rhythm at the 45 s mark with a new visual mode.
- Weakness: after 22 s the buildings are small and crowded, so tags at 360 px are hard to read.
