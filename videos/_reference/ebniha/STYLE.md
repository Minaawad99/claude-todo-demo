# «ابنيها بنفسك» — measured style guide

From 20 reference videos (Saudi-dialect "build it yourself" shorts, 720×1280, 65–116 s). Per-video detail is in
`notes/vNN.md`, the spoken text in `transcripts.txt`. We take the method; we never copy a script or a drawing.

## 1. The idea

The viewer builds a real thing step by step. It starts as a crude version anyone could make, each flaw gets one
engineering fix, and every fix adds one visible part to the same object until it is the real thing.

Topics seen: cell tower, metro, glass factory, dam, rocket, a state, aircraft toilet, airport baggage system, soft-serve
machine, concrete mixer, car wash, tower crane, escalator, garbage truck, electric pole, fridge, elevator, pool, shower,
asphalt road.

## 2. Script

| measure | range | typical |
|---|---|---|
| length | 65–116 s | 75–90 s |
| words | 160–307 | about 200 |
| sentences | 15–37 | 20–26 |
| pace, pauses included | 2.4–2.8 words/s | 2.6 |
| problem → fix rounds | 5–9 | 6 |

Fixed order (same in all 20):

1. **Hook, 0–4 s.** «لو <crude action>… صار عندك <thing> بدائي!». No greeting, no title, no series name.
2. **First problem, by 4–7 s.** «بس المشكلة إن…» with a concrete failure, often a number («تحتاج 300 سطل»).
3. **Rounds.** Problem («بس…» / «لكن…») then fix («عشان كذا…» / «فـ…»). In the transcripts: «عشان كذا» 55 times,
   «لكن» 33, «بس المشكلة» 15. Each fix is one sentence naming what you add and why it works.
4. **Naming.** After a fix, the part's real name: «وهذا يسمونه…» / «اسمه…» (32 times). The viewer learns the term
   after seeing what it does.
5. **Odd fact, about 45–62 s.** «وتدري وش الغريب؟» (12 of 20), one surprising fact, usually with a number.
6. **Finishing touches.** «باقي تركب … و… و…» (16 of 20): three small parts in one sentence.
7. **Payoff.** «ومبروك عليك، بنيت <thing> بنفسك!»
8. **Optional ask** (8 of 20): «اكتب فكرتك، وأكثر فكرة تجيب لايكات بتكون الفيديو الجاي.»

Wording: second person throughout («تحفر، تركب، تصب»), present tense, short sentences, everyday comparisons («مثل
برغي عملاق»), real numbers with units, no filler.

## 3. Voice and sound

- **Voice:** one young adult male, clean dry studio sound, no reverb. Energetic and friendly, a steady brisk rhythm.
- **Intonation:** pitch rises on the problem («بس المشكلة…»), comes down settled on the fix («عشان كذا…»), a small
  lift on the part's name, real surprise on «وتدري وش الغريب؟», a proud, happy «ومبروك».
- **Pauses:** about 0.25 s between sentences (measured 0.24–0.30 s average), longest pause about 1.2 s before the odd
  fact or the payoff. It never stops inside a sentence.
- **Loudness:** −17 LUFS integrated, loudness range 1.6–2.6 LU (very even).
- **Music:** one upbeat electronic / synth-pop loop, steady 4/4, from the first frame to the last, clearly under the
  voice, never changing.
- **Effects:** one short real-world sound per part as it lands (digging, metal clank, motor whir, water, hiss, beep),
  a buzzer or crash on a failure, a pop when a tag appears, a party pop with confetti on «ومبروك».

## 4. Picture

- **Full-bleed vertical frame.** No panel, no title bar, no logo.
- **Side view with a cut.** The build stands on a ground line; soil in cross-section fills the bottom 25–50% so
  foundations, pipes and tunnels show. Machines get a see-through skin or x-ray ghosting when the inside matters.
- **One object, one continuous take.** The object stays centred and only grows; parts from earlier steps stay. The
  camera holds 2–4 s, then eases in to the new part or back out. Hard cuts only for cutaways (molecule, macro, night).
- **A new element every 2–4 s.** Parts drop from above, stack, slide in or pop with a small overshoot.
- **Problems are acted out** before the fix: the mast falls, the pool floats up, the milk goes green, the car sinks.
- **Render families:** flat 2D vector with gradients, grain and dark outlines (13 of 20), low-poly 3D with a photo sky
  (5), 2.5D (2). The series identity is the script loop and the captions, not the render look.
- **Setting:** local and sunny: sand, palms, low houses, a man in a thobe. Sky blue + sand + one or two accents.
- **Labels (2D videos):** dark rounded pill above the part, cream or yellow text, about 2 s; red text for a problem.
  «… بدائي!» pill at 2.5–3.5 s. Numbers sit on in-world dials, screens and counters, coloured by meaning
  (red danger, green safe). The 3D videos have almost no labels and are hard to follow muted, so we always label.
- **Odd-fact card:** scene dims, a cream rounded card with a small comparison drawing and one big number.

## 5. Captions

One word at a time, heavy rounded Arabic face, white with a thick dark outline, centred, no box, no colour highlight,
each word popping in with the voice. At y ≈ 72% in 14 videos, at y ≈ 25–28% in 6 (v15–v20).

## 6. Ending

«باقي…» parts land quickly, then a wide pull-back on the finished build for «ومبروك», confetti in most. The video ends
1–3 s after the last word. Three closers seen:
- none (v12–v20);
- vote card 2 s after «ومبروك» (v04–v11): scene dims, a speech-bubble «اكتب فكرتك!», four rows of next builds with
  heart counters ticking up, 4–6 s;
- a mid-video interrupt before the finale with a pause icon and a "link in bio" card (v01–v03). We do not use this one.
