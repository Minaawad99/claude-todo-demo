## What it is, in one paragraph

A realistic 3D build-along. One object is built in place on a dusty concrete floor in a hangar with a corrugated metal
wall. It starts from a silly household idea (a firecracker under a can of beans = "an engine"), then every problem
(«بس…») gets a fix («فـ…») that adds one real part, and the part is named («ده الكرنك»). 45 short lines, about 163 words
in 78 s of voice, no pauses at all. It ends with the parts flying together into the finished thing, an orbit around it
and «مبروك يا باشمهندس».

## Motion

- No hard cuts in 80 s. The camera never stops: slow dolly-in on each new part, orbit or crane between rounds, a quick
  push for a punch line, a full 360° orbit at the end, then fade to black.
- Parts arrive by a short fly-in or drop with a little bounce, or by a "morph" (the can turns into the piston).
- Transparent glass cylinders show the insides, so nothing has to be cut away.
- States are shown on the object itself: the cylinder head glows orange as the temperature climbs, blue water runs in
  the hoses, red wave rings leave the exhaust, the engine shakes on «بتترعش».
- Counters on the top tag count up live (×1 → ×60 فرقعة / ثانية, 146 → 250 °C).
