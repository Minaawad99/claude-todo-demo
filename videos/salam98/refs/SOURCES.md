## Facts
- English Wikipedia «MS al-Salam Boccaccio 98» and the Panama Maritime Authority report as summarised in search results:
  built 1970 in Italy, rebuilt taller in 1991, Duba to Safaga, about 1,400 aboard and about 220 vehicles, fire on the car
  deck, blocked scuppers and fire-fighting water, capsized about 01:33 on 3 February 2006, 388 survivors, more than 1,000
  dead, automatic beacon picked up at RAF Kinloss and passed on through France, owner acquitted in 2008 and sentenced to
  seven years in 2009.
- From memory, not confirmed by a source opened in this session: the second ferry of the same company that did not go to
  help and its master's six-month sentence; the black box recovered "weeks later"; rescue arriving "after long hours".
