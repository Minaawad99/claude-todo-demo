## Facts (checked)
- A whole-blood donation is about 1 pint (≈ 470–500 ml) and takes about 8–10 minutes; an adult has about 10 pints. — American Red Cross.
- Plasma volume back within about 24–48 hours. — American Red Cross; NHS Blood and Transplant.
- Low oxygen at the kidney → erythropoietin (EPO) → more red cells from the marrow. — Guyton & Hall, ch. 33.
- About 2 million red cells made per second; a red cell lives about 120 days (≈ 1% replaced a day). — Guyton & Hall.
- Red cells back in about 4–6 weeks; iron takes longer; whole blood can be given every 56 days (8 weeks) in the US. — American Red Cross.
- One donation can help save up to three lives (red cells, plasma, platelets). — American Red Cross.
