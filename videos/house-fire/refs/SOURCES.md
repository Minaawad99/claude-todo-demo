# تهرب من حريقة إزاي؟ — sources

No photos used; the flat, the stairwell and the block of flats are generic cartoon drawings.

Facts checked:
- Feel the door and the knob with the back of your hand before opening; if hot, keep it shut and use another way out;
  get low and crawl under smoke; close doors behind you; use the stairs, never the lift; if trapped, close the door,
  block the gaps with towels or sheets, signal from the window and call the fire brigade (NFPA "Escape planning",
  US Fire Administration, American Red Cross home fire safety).
- Most fire deaths are caused by smoke and toxic gases rather than burns (NFPA, USFA).
- UL Fire Safety Research Institute, "Close Before You Doze": a bedroom behind a closed door stayed around 100 °F
  (about 38 °C) while the open-door room exceeded 1,000 °F (over 500 °C).
- You may have as little as two minutes to get out once the smoke alarm sounds (NFPA, American Red Cross).
- Sleeping people are not reliably woken by smells, including smoke (Carskadon et al., Brown University, 2004); smoke
  alarms are needed for that.
- Fire brigade emergency number in Egypt: 180.
