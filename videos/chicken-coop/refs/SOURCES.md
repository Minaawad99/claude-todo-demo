# عشة فراخ على السطوح — sources

Idea from a video the user uploaded (an English 3D build-along of a backyard chicken coop). Ours is made different on
purpose: an Egyptian **rooftop** coop, cats and the weasel (ابن عرس) instead of fox/raccoon, summer heat instead of
winter frost, and extra steps the original doesn't have (roost bar, nest boxes, the no-rooster surprise, the light lamp).
No line is translated from it.

Facts checked:
- Chicken wire keeps hens in but doesn't keep predators out; 1/2-inch hardware cloth is the usual weasel/cat-proof mesh;
  small weasels pass ~7/8-inch (≈2 cm) gaps: https://backyardpoultry.iamcountryside.com/coops/chicken-fences/ ,
  https://backyardchickens.com/threads/how-small-a-hole-is-weasel-proof.578222
- Chickens have no sweat glands, cool by panting; heat stress starts above ~27 °C and is clear by ~29–30 °C, laying drops:
  https://extension.psu.edu/hot-weather-management-of-poultry , https://fieldreport.caes.uga.edu/?p=20267
- Hens ovulate every 24–26 h with or without a rooster; the rooster only makes eggs fertile:
  https://livestock.extension.wisc.edu/articles/life-cycle-of-a-laying-hen , https://australianeggs.org.au/farming/how-chickens-make-eggs
- Hens need ~14–16 h of light a day to keep laying; short winter days stop them; a lamp in the early morning fixes it:
  https://extension.psu.edu/artificial-lighting-for-winter-egg-production , https://pubs.ext.vt.edu/2902/2902-1097.html
- One nest box per 4–5 hens, hens roost on a bar at night, dust bathing in sand/dirt: common extension guidance.
