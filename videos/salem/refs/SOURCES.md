## Facts (checked online)
English Wikipedia «MV Salem Express», Divernet, Masrawy (2016): struck a reef about 23:13 on Saturday 14 December 1991 on the way
from Jeddah to Safaga; the reef holed the forward starboard bow and knocked the bow door open, flooding the car deck; sank in about
10–20 minutes; lies on her starboard side in about 32 m; official toll about 470, likely higher; the master (named Hassan Moro in
one source) took a shortcut not authorised at night, since banned for large ships. Distances computed from the wreck position:
15 km to Safaga, about 180 km to Duba, about 790 km Jeddah–Safaga in a straight line.
