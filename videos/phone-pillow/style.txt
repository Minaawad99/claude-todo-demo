Warm, confident and curious, with real amazement on the surprising facts and numbers, and a little urgency on the danger. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Make the opening warning "اوعى" sharp and gripping and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
