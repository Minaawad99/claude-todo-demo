Style: a «اوعى…» warning video (the user's idea, after a "don't pour water on burning oil" video), built in the channel's cartoon panel.

Facts:
- Li-ion cells: rolled layers of cathode, anode and a thin polymer separator soaked in a flammable organic electrolyte; charging
  moves lithium ions anode-ward and makes heat.
- Separator melts around 120–140 °C → internal short → more heat → thermal runaway (USPTO thermal-protection patent; Volta
  Foundation "Separators and their role in safety"; Battery Technology "What is thermal runaway").
  https://volta.foundation/lithium-ion-battery-separators-and-their-role-in-safety/
  https://www.batterytechonline.com/testing-safety/what-is-thermal-runaway-here-s-a-battery-of-answers
- Layered-oxide cathodes release oxygen when they break down, which feeds the fire and makes it hard to put out.
- Apple: iPhone works best at 0–35 °C ambient temperature (support.apple.com, "About iPhone operating temperature").
- Samsung Galaxy Note 7 recall (2016): about 2.5 million phones, battery fires.
- Fire services warn against charging under pillows / in bed (heat can't escape, bedding burns): SlashGear, Tucson Fire (KGUN9),
  Lincolnshire Fire & Rescue (Sleaford Standard).
  https://www.slashgear.com/can-charging-my-phone-under-my-pillow-cause-a-fire-10557509/
  https://www.kgun9.com/news/local-news/tucson-fire-says-dont-charge-phone-under-pillow
