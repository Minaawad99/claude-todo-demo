# Pregnancy episode: references

Visual references only (looked at, never reused):
- "Blastocyst English.svg", Wikimedia Commons: outer cell ring (trophoblast), inner cell mass, cavity.
- "9-Week Human Embryo from Ectopic Pregnancy.jpg" (Ed Uthman, CC BY), Wikimedia Commons: big head, eye spot,
  curled body, hands and feet forming. Used for the 10-week fetus shape.
- "Brain human sagittal section.svg", Wikimedia Commons: cortex, corpus callosum, brainstem, cerebellum. The vomiting
  centre (area postrema) sits on the back of the medulla, at the floor of the fourth ventricle.
- «طب إزاي؟» reference sheets v17 (womb, fetus, placenta, cord) and v11 (brain mid-section), for the hand.

Facts checked: blastocyst reaches the uterus about day 5 (100+ cells) and implants about days 6–10; hCG doubles about every
48–72 h early on; heartbeat visible about week 6 of pregnancy; organs formed by about week 10 (fetus ~3 cm, strawberry);
nausea peaks about week 9 and eases after the first trimester; GDF15 from the fetal side of the placenta acts on the
brainstem, and women with low pre-pregnancy GDF15 are more sensitive (Fejzo et al., Nature 2023); progesterone relaxes
vessel walls and blood pressure falls in early pregnancy; food aversions to meat, eggs, coffee as protection during
organogenesis (Flaxman & Sherman 2000, a hypothesis); pica for ice or clay can signal iron deficiency; nausea in early
pregnancy is linked with a lower risk of miscarriage (Hinkle et al., JAMA Intern Med 2016).
