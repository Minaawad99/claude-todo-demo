# «تحويل المسار والحموضة» — sources

## Facts (general medical knowledge, consistent with ASMBS / SAGES guidance; no new searches in this session)
- Roux-en-Y gastric bypass is the preferred bariatric operation for patients with obesity and significant GERD.
- Sleeve gastrectomy can worsen GERD or cause new reflux (high pressure in the narrow tube); severe reflux after a sleeve is usually treated by conversion to a bypass.
- The bypass pouch is made from the cardia, which has few acid-producing (parietal) cells; acid is made in the body of the remnant stomach, which is closed off.
- The Roux limb (about 100–150 cm) keeps bile far from the oesophagus; the small pouch empties fast; weight loss lowers abdominal pressure; a hiatal hernia is often repaired in the same operation. Many patients stop acid-suppressing medicines afterwards.
