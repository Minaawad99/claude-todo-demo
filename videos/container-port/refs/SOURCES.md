# Sources — «تبني ميناء حاويات إزاي؟»

Style: «لو» (`videos/_reference/law/`). The topic comes from a video the user uploaded (a Gulf-Arabic «ابنيها بنفسك» port
video). It was transcribed only to make ours different on purpose. Theirs: mooring post → 16 m draft → dredger + new land
from the sand → breakwater with star blocks → piles, quay and rubber fenders → pilot, tugs and bollards → quay crane with a
four-corner spreader, a box every two minutes → yard stacked 5 high → surprise "24,000 boxes = 140 km in a row" →
lighthouse and red/green buoys → rail, customs, warehouses.
Ours avoids their breakwater, fenders, tugs/pilot, lighthouse/buoys, new land and their surprise, and adds: the standard
box itself, the crane's weight as the reason for the piles, silting and re-dredging, the box number and the computer,
reefer plugs for Egyptian oranges, the stowage plan, boxes lost at sea and lashing, gate cameras / X-ray / rail.
The draft (≈16 m) and channel (≈18 m) numbers are the same physical facts in both; the wording and drawing are ours.

- Loaded ultra-large container ships draw ≈ 16–17 m. https://transportgeography.org/contents/chapter5/maritime-transportation/draft-containership-capacity/
- East Port Said access channel dredged to ≈ 18–18.5 m. https://www.apmterminals.com/en/scct/media/news/2015/scct-to-gain-24-hour-vessel-accessibility
- A 20 ft box's maximum gross mass is 30,480 kg (ISO 668) → «ممكن يوزن تلاتين طن».
- Quay cranes: ≈ 25–38 lifts per hour (marlog.aast.edu/archive/2023/proceedings/Marlog12_paper_27.pdf); weight ≈ 800–2,000 t,
  TT Club gives 1,000–1,200 t (https://www.ttclub.com/fileadmin/uploads/tt-club/Documents/Critical_asset_series/TT-Club-Critical-Assets-Crane-FINAL.pdf); new piling for crane rails (https://www.ajot.com/news/new-cranes-and-existing-docks).
- Channels silt up and need maintenance dredging (standard practice; Boskalis https://boskalis.com/activities/dredging).
- RTG yards stack 5–6 high (https://hyster.com/... "It takes 2" white paper; porteconomicsmanagement.org stacking density).
- Box number ISO 6346: 3-letter owner code + category letter (U) + 6 digits + check digit = 4 letters, 7 digits. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_6346
- Egypt is the world's largest orange exporter (since 2019). https://www.freshplaza.com/article/9448056/egypt-remains-the-world-s-largest-orange-exporter/ ;
  Ain Sokhna reefer berths with 584 and 320 plugs. https://www.freshplaza.com/article/9829932/egyptian-citrus-exports-rely-on-ain-sokhna-port-logistics/
- Stowage: heavy boxes not over light ones; loading computer checks stability. https://www.steamshipmutual.com/containerised-cargo-stowage-and-securing-risk-alert-74-0
- Boxes lost at sea: WSC average 1,566 a year (2008–2022), 10-year average 1,274 → «أكتر من ألف». https://www.worldshipping.org/s/Containers_Lost_at_Sea_2023_FINAL-t3c8.pdf
- Twist locks between tiers, lashing bars to the lower tiers. https://www.ukpandi.com/media/files/imports/13108/bulletins/5986-container-lashing-web.pdf
- Not used: the 1956 Ideal X cost figure ($5.83 → $0.16 a ton) — true, left out for length.
