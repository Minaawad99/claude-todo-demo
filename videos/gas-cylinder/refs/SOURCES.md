# Sources — «أوعى تقلب الأنبوبة!» (tilting a butane/LPG cylinder)

- Used on its side, the cylinder can feed liquid LPG to the valve, which is made for vapour; liquid can damage the valve's
  O-ring; frost on the cylinder means liquid is drawn too fast or is low:
  - https://www.staffnet.manchester.ac.uk/media/eps/chemistry-intranet/health-and-safety/Know-Your-LPG-v2.pdf
  - https://www.qld.gov.au/emergency/safety/gas/cylinder-safety
  - https://www.worksafe.act.gov.au/health-and-safety-portal/safety-topics/dangerous-goods-and-hazardous-substances/liquid-petroleum-gas-lpg-safety
- Safety advice (keep upright and ventilated, soapy water for leaks, no flames or switches when gas smells, close the valve,
  open windows):
  - https://legacy.doe.gov.ph/sites/default/files/pdf/consumer_connect/lpg_safety_tips.pdf
  - https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/ask-us/prevent-gas-cylinder-accidents-simple-steps-to-ensure-safe-lpg-use-in-your-home-1.1720197996472
- General knowledge: LPG vapour is heavier than air; liquid LPG expands roughly 230–270 times into gas (said as «ميتين مرة وأكتر»).
