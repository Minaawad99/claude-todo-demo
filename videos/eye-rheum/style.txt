Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend why we wake up with crusty sleep in the corner of our eyes. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Brisk, flowing conversational pace, never dragging. Take only a very quick breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like العماص, الدموع, الزيت, المخاط, خمستاشر مرة, الرمش, بنعيط, لأ, رجل الرمل. Make the opening line intriguing and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
