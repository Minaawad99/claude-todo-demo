Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident teacher explaining to a friend how long the blood vessels in your body really are. Warm and clear, more teacher than hype, with quiet amazement on the surprising facts and numbers. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like مرتين ونص, غلط, الشعيرات, سبعة ميكرون, مية ألف كيلو, تسعة آلاف, لأ. Make the opening line intriguing and the question "إيه الحكاية؟" playful. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
