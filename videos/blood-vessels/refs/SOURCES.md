## Facts (checked)
- Krogh's 100,000 km figure (1922, *The Anatomy and Physiology of Capillaries*), worked out on "an extraordinarily large person" with 50 kg of muscle and a capillary density that was too high; the recent estimate is 9,000–19,000 km:
  Poole DC, Kano Y, Koga S, Musch TI. "August Krogh: Muscle capillary function and oxygen delivery." *Comp Biochem Physiol A* 253:110852 (2021). doi:10.1016/j.cbpa.2020.110852 (as cited in Wikipedia «Capillary» and «August Krogh»).
- Krogh: Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine 1920, for his work on the capillaries (nobelprize.org).
- Capillary 5–10 µm across; red cells about 7.5 µm, so they pass in single file; the wall is one layer of endothelial cells (Wikipedia «Capillary»).
- Human hair about 50–100 µm (≈70 µm) → a capillary is about 10× thinner.
- Adult blood volume about 5 L; at rest the heart pumps about 5 L a minute, so the blood goes round in about a minute.
- Aorta about 2.5 cm across (a garden hose).
- Earth's circumference 40,075 km → 100,000 km ≈ 2.5 times round; 19,000 km is less than half.
- Cairo → Tokyo ≈ 9,500 km, Cairo → Sydney ≈ 14,400 km (great-circle).
- The cornea has no blood vessels; it takes its oxygen from the air through the tear film (and from the aqueous humour).
