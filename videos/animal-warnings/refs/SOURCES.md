# Sources — حيوانات شعرت بالخطر قبل وقوع الكوارث

1. Khao Lak elephants, 26 Dec 2004 — widely reported (mahout accounts, National Geographic 2005 "Did animals sense tsunami was coming?").
2. L'Aquila toads 2009 — Grant & Halliday, Journal of Zoology 2010: common toads abandoned the breeding site 5 days before the quake.
3. Haicheng 1975 — snakes emerging in winter and freezing, evacuation hours before the M7.3 (USGS / Wang et al. 2006, BSSA).
4. Golden-winged warblers 2014 — Streby et al., Current Biology, DOI 10.1016/j.cub.2014.10.079; left >24 h before the April 2014 outbreak (35 deaths).
5. Etna goats — Wikelski, Max Planck Institute of Animal Behavior: https://www.animal-sensors.mpg.de/11706/a-four-legged-early-warning-system — restless hours before eruptions (4–6 h; some reports up to 14 h).
6. Mount Pelée 1902 — Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1902_eruption_of_Mount_Pel%C3%A9e ; popular accounts of snakes and insects invading (death count unverified → «تقول الروايات»).
7. Blacktip sharks, Tropical Storm Gabrielle 2001 — Heupel, Simpfendorfer & Hueter 2003, "Running before the storm" (Mote Marine Laboratory).
8. Italian farm animals — Wikelski et al. 2020, Ethology; ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2020/07/200706101837.htm — up to 20 h before. Some seismologists sceptical.
9. Red wood ants — Berberich, University of Duisburg-Essen, 2013 (NBC/LiveScience): https://www.nbcnews.com/news/all/ant-icipation-insects-know-when-earthquakes-are-coming-flna1C9323346
10. Yala National Park 2004 — park officials reported almost no dead animals (BBC, National Geographic 2005).
11. Flamingos, Point Calimere (India) 2004 — press reports only → «تقول التقارير».
12. Mianzhu toads before the 2008 Sichuan quake — press reports; officials called it normal.
13. Helike 373 BC — Aelian, On Animals 11.19 → «يروي».
14. Namazu legend; Tokyo Metropolitan Government catfish study 1976–1992 (weak link) → hedged.
15. Tangshan 1976 — Chinese reports of fish and livestock behaviour → «تقول التقارير».
