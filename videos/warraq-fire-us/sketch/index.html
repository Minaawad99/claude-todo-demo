1. Leave the car and get far away, upwind.
2. Call 911 (or your local number) and give the exact location.
3. Clothes on fire: stop, drop and roll, then cover with cotton or a blanket.
4. Burns: cool running water for 20 minutes. No toothpaste, no ice.
