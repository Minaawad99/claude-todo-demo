Speak in casual Egyptian Arabic (Cairo dialect), like a calm, confident friend daring the listener to try a quick trick with their own hand, then explaining why it works. Warm and playful at the start, with a little suspense before "ارفع البنصر", a satisfied smile on "سهلة" and "ولا مشكلة", and quiet amazement on the explanation. Natural, relaxed conversational speed, not rushed. Take a short natural breath between sentences, and don't stop at commas. Emphasize key words like البنصر, لازق, الوسطاني, شريط, عضلة. Make the opening line intriguing. Never use Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation; pronounce ق as a glottal stop like Cairenes.
