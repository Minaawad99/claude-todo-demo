# «ليه البنصر مش بيترفع؟» — sources

## Idea and structure
- The user sent a short English video ("Try it before you scroll: which finger got stuck") and asked for the same hook,
  script order and hand-driven story in the «اسكتش» style, in Egyptian Arabic. The script was translated, not copied
  shot for shot; every drawing here is our own.

## Anatomy (checked)
- Extensor digitorum: one muscle with tendons to the index, middle, ring and little fingers; the middle and ring
  fingers have no extensor of their own.
- Juncturae tendinum: bands between the extensor tendons on the back of the hand, just above the knuckles. The one
  between the middle and ring tendons is the strongest. (Gray's Anatomy, "Extensor digitorum"; Wikipedia,
  "Juncturae tendinum".)
- Extensor indicis and extensor digiti minimi: the extra extensors of the index and the little finger, which let them
  straighten on their own. (Gray's Anatomy; Wikipedia, "Extensor indicis muscle", "Extensor digiti minimi muscle".)
- With the middle finger flexed, its tendon is pulled toward the fingertip and, through the junctura, holds the ring
  finger's tendon too, so the ring finger cannot be lifted off the table (the well-known "stuck ring finger" trick).
- Pianists and the ring finger: the ring finger is the least independent finger, a known problem in piano technique
  (finger independence studies, e.g. Häger-Ross & Schieber 2000, J. Neurosci. 20(22):8542).

## Drawing
- The hand rig (three phalanges, joints wider than the shafts, nails on the distal half, back-of-hand tendons, knuckle
  domes, web shadows, lift by foreshortening with the shadow left on the table) was approved by the user as a test sheet
  before the episode was built.
